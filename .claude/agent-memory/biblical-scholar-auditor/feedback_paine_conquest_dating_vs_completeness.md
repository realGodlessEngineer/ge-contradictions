**Verification result:** Paine is REAL (d. 1809), Part II is real, year 1795 defensible (preface signed Oct 1795 — do NOT flag year), and he DOES cite the exact verses (Josh 15:63 Jebusites + Judg 1). He does NOT frame it as the internal triumphalist-vs-realistic contradiction (total conquest claims belied by incomplete conquest), and he never engages Josh 21:44 "delivered all their enemies into their hand."
