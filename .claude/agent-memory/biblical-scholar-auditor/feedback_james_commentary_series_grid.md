When NT entries cite "Blomberg, Moo, and Davids in the Pillar/NIGTC/WBC commentaries on James" or similar series-author trios, verify each pairing against this grid:

| Author | Series | Year | Publisher |
|---|---|---|---|
| Allison, Dale C. Jr. | ICC | 2013 | Bloomsbury T&T Clark |
| Blomberg, Craig L. & Kamell, Mariam J. | ZECNT | 2008 | Zondervan |
| Davids, Peter H. | NIGTC | 1982 | Eerdmans |
| Johnson, Luke Timothy | Anchor Bible 37A | 1995 | Doubleday |
| Martin, Ralph P. | WBC 48 | 1988 | Word/Thomas Nelson |
| McKnight, Scot | NICNT | 2011 | Eerdmans |
| Moo, Douglas J. | Pillar (PNTC) | 2000 | Eerdmans |
| Moo, Douglas J. | TNTC (older) | 1985 | Eerdmans |

**Common AI miscombinations** (seen in batch 20):
- "Blomberg in Pillar" — WRONG; Blomberg is ZECNT
- "Blomberg in NIGTC" — WRONG; Blomberg is ZECNT
- "Davids in WBC" — WRONG; Davids is NIGTC; Martin is WBC
- "Moo in NIGTC" — WRONG; Moo is Pillar
- "Allison in Hermeneia" — WRONG; James has no Hermeneia volume; Allison is ICC

**Why:** AI groups together evangelical James commentators and may scramble their series. Pillar/NIGTC/WBC are all evangelical-leaning Greek-text commentaries, and the AI confuses which scholar is in which.

**How to apply:** Verify author-series pairs against this grid before passing scholarship list. If batch cites multiple James commentators, expect at least one misattribution unless explicitly verified.
