Richard S. Hess, "The Jericho and Ai of the Book of Joshua" is in *Critical Issues in Early Israelite History*, ed. R. S. Hess, G. A. Klingbeil, and P. J. Ray Jr., Bulletin for Biblical Research Supplement 3 (Winona Lake: Eisenbrauns, 2008), 33-46.

It is NOT in J. K. Hoffmeier and A. R. Millard, eds., *The Future of Biblical Archaeology* (Eerdmans 2004), despite that volume being a similar Hoffmeier-edited apologetic volume from the same era.

**Why:** Pass 3 of batch_12 caught entry 236 attributing Hess's Jericho/Ai essay to the wrong edited volume. The two volumes are easy to confuse (both are conservative/apologetic edited volumes on Israelite history/archaeology with overlapping contributors). Page numbers 33-46 are correct for both attributions, which is what made the error hide.

**How to apply:** When citing Hess "Jericho and Ai," verify it is BBR Supplement 3 (Eisenbrauns 2008). Be alert generally for AI drafts conflating Hoffmeier/Millard's *Future of Biblical Archaeology* (Eerdmans 2004) with Hess/Klingbeil/Ray's *Critical Issues in Early Israelite History* (Eisenbrauns 2008) — they share an apologetic-Israelite-history scope and similar contributor pool.
