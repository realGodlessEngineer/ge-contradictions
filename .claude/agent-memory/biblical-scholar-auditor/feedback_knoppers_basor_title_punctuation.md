Knoppers's 1993 BASOR article is titled "Treaty, Tribute List, or Diplomatic Letter: KTU 3.1 Reexamined" — with a **colon** before "KTU 3.1 Reexamined," not a question mark. BASOR 289 (1993), pp. 81-94.

**Why:** AI drafts (and human ones) frequently render the title with a question mark because the main title is a tri-option question ("Treaty, Tribute List, or Diplomatic Letter?"). The published title uses a colon. JSTOR/University of Chicago Press journal records confirm: colon.

**How to apply:** When auditing Knoppers BASOR 289 citations, check for question mark vs colon between "Letter" and "KTU 3.1." Fix question mark to colon if found. This is the same kind of subtitle-punctuation drift previously seen in pass-7 late audits.
