H. G. M. Williamson's two well-known commentaries are easily conflated by AI drafts:
- **1 and 2 Chronicles** = New Century Bible Commentary (Eerdmans/Marshall, Morgan & Scott, 1982), ~447 pp
- **Ezra, Nehemiah** = Word Biblical Commentary 16 (Word, 1985), ~417 pp

WBC 14 = 1 Chronicles is by Roddy Braun (1986); WBC 15 = 2 Chronicles is by Raymond Dillard (1987). **There is no Williamson WBC Chronicles.**

**Why:** AI drafts confuse Williamson's two commentaries because the same scholar wrote both volumes in different series. Pass 5 of batch_16 caught entry 308 commentary saying "Hugh Williamson's older Word Biblical Commentary on Chronicles" — this is wrong; it should be NCB.

**How to apply:** When commentary references "Williamson's Chronicles commentary" verify it's cited as NCB/NCBC. When it references "Williamson's Ezra-Nehemiah commentary" verify it's WBC 16. Cross-check commentary prose against scholarship list to ensure series consistency. If scholarship entry says NCB but commentary says WBC, flag the commentary text.
