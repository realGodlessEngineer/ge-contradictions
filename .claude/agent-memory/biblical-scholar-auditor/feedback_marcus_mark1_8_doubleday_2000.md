Joel Marcus, Mark 1-8, AB 27 first edition (2000) is published by **Doubleday**, not Yale University Press. The Anchor Bible series transferred to Yale UP in 2007 (AB→AYB rebrand). The Yale UP paperback reprint of Marcus Mark 1-8 is 2009, not 2000.

**Why:** AI drafts conflate the AB-era 2000 first edition with the later Yale UP reprint. Found in batch_25 entries 486, 487, 488, 489 — all cited "Anchor Bible 27. Yale University Press, 2000" which is anachronistic by 7 years.

**How to apply:** When verifying Marcus Mark 1-8 (2000), always check: publisher = Doubleday; series = Anchor Bible (AB) 27. For the 2009 Yale UP reprint, use Anchor Yale Bible (AYB) 27 if citing the reprint. The original 2000 first edition predates the rebrand.
