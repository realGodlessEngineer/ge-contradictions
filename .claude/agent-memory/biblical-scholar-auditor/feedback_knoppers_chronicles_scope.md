Knoppers wrote only the Anchor Bible volumes on 1 Chronicles: AB 12 (*1 Chronicles 1-9*, 2003/2004) and AB 12A (*1 Chronicles 10-29*, 2004), continuous pagination 1-1045. He did NOT write a commentary on 2 Chronicles. Klein wrote the Hermeneia commentaries on 1 Chronicles (2006) and 2 Chronicles (2012).

**Why:** Pass-1 AI scholarship drafts in this project repeatedly attributed "Knoppers in his Hermeneia 1 Chronicles" or "Knoppers in his Chronicler studies" to 2 Chronicles passages where his commentary doesn't apply. The two errors compound: wrong series (Knoppers is AB, not Hermeneia) and wrong volume scope (1 Chr only, not 2 Chr).

**How to apply:** Whenever Knoppers is cited for a 2 Chronicles passage, either (a) remove the named-work claim and replace with his journal/essay literature on the Chronicler if relevant, (b) drop Knoppers and use Klein/Japhet/Dillard for 2 Chr instead, or (c) verify a specific Knoppers journal article that addresses the pattern. Klein's 2 Chronicles Hermeneia (2012) is the correct Hermeneia volume for 2 Chr commentary.
