Knoppers AB 12A (1 Chronicles 10-29) pagination begins at p. 516 (continuing from AB 12) and runs through p. 1045. Early pages 516-575 cover 1 Chronicles 10-11 territory: Saul's death, David's anointing, capture of Jerusalem, and David's mighty men. Maachah/Absalom genealogy material is in 1 Chr 3, which is in AB 12 (1 Chr 1-9), NOT AB 12A.

**Why:** Pass-2 audit of batch_14 found AB 12A pp. 567-570 cited for a Maachah-daughter-of-Absalom discussion in 1 Kgs 15:2 / 2 Chr 13:2 context — those pages don't address that material. Knoppers cited the Davidic genealogy (1 Chr 3) only in AB 12.

**How to apply:** When AI-drafted scholarship cites Knoppers AB 12A pp. 516-580 for any topic, sanity-check that the topic genuinely concerns 1 Chr 10-11 (Saul's death, David's rise to kingship, Jerusalem capture, mighty men, ark to Jerusalem). For any Davidic-genealogy/wives/sons topic, the correct Knoppers volume is AB 12 (1 Chr 3), not AB 12A.
