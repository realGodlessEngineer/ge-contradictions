When commentary on Synoptic-vs-Johannine call narratives (John 1:35-42 vs Mark 1:16-20 / Luke 5:1-11) attributes the two-stage call harmonization to "Carson, Köstenberger, Blomberg" or similar trio, the Köstenberger BECNT John citation is often the missing entry.

**Why:** AI scholar enrichment treats Carson Pillar and Blomberg as primary apologetic harmonization authors and lists those, but the BECNT volume by Köstenberger (a major evangelical John scholar) is a substantive third name in the joint thesis attribution and should not be passed over.

**How to apply:** When an entry's commentary names Köstenberger by series ("BECNT") for a John passage and the scholarship omits him, add: `Köstenberger, Andreas J. John. Baker Exegetical Commentary on the New Testament. Grand Rapids: Baker Academic, 2004, [pp.].` Köstenberger's BECNT John was first published 2004 by Baker Academic (720 pp). Watch especially for entries on John 1 (call narrative), John 11 (Lazarus prayer), John 14-16 (Farewell Discourse), and John 21 (epilogue).
