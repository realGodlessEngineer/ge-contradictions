In Brown's *Death of the Messiah* (Doubleday 1994), the primary discussions of Judas Iscariot are:
- Vol 1, §29 "Judas, the Chief Priests, and the Price of Innocent Blood" (Matt 27:3-10), pp. 734-761
- Vol 2, pp. 1394-1418 — Brown's appendix-style treatment of Judas's death and the broader Judas traditions

The page ranges pp. 209-213 and pp. 256-259 in Vol 1 are within Act I Gethsemane prayer sections (§6-§10, pp. 189-265), NOT Judas content. AI drafts often cite these as "Judas traditions" — they're miscitations.

Brown DM Vol 1 TOC confirmed via Internet Archive: §6-§10 = pp. 189-265 = Gethsemane prayer; §29 = pp. 734-761 = Judas/chief priests.
