In Brown's *Death of the Messiah* (Doubleday 1994), the primary discussions of Judas Iscariot are:
- Vol 1, §29 "Judas, the Chief Priests, and the Price of Innocent Blood" (Matt 27:3-10), pp. 734-761
- Vol 2, pp. 1394-1418 — Brown's appendix-style treatment of Judas's death and the broader Judas traditions

The page ranges pp. 209-213 and pp. 256-259 in Vol 1 are within Act I Gethsemane prayer sections (§6-§10, pp. 189-265), NOT Judas content. AI drafts often cite these as "Judas traditions" — they're miscitations.

**Why:** Audit of batch_26 entry 511 (Satan entered Judas) in 2026-05-18 audit found an AI-drafted cite "Vol. 1, pp. 209-213, 256-259 on Judas traditions" that was wrong. Brown DM Vol 1 TOC confirmed via Internet Archive: §6-§10 = pp. 189-265 = Gethsemane prayer; §29 = pp. 734-761 = Judas/chief priests.

**How to apply:** When seeing "Brown DM Vol 1 pp. 200-260 on Judas" in AI drafts, flag and rewrite to use §29 (pp. 734-761) for the Matthean Judas scene, plus Vol 2 pp. 1394-1418 for the death-of-Judas appendix and broader traditions. Add to Brown DM page-anchor list.
