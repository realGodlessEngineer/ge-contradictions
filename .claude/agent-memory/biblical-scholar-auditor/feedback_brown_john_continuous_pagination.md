Brown's Anchor Bible John uses CONTINUOUS pagination across the two volumes:
- Vol 29 (John I-XII, 1966): cxlvi + 538 pages (commentary pages ~1-538)
- Vol 29A (John XIII-XXI, 1970): xvi + 670 pages (continues from p. 539 to p. 1208)

So when verifying page anchors:
- Citations for John 1-12 should be in pp. 1-538 (Vol 29)
- Citations for John 13-21 should be in pp. 539-1208 (Vol 29A)
- John 20:17 area ~pp. 992-994, 1011-1017
- John 20:19-29 ~pp. 1019-1049
- John 13:21-30 ~pp. 571-580
- Anointing at Bethany John 12 ~pp. 449-454

**Why:** During batch_26 audit (2026-05-18), confirmed via Internet Archive that Brown AB John has continuous pagination. Vol 29A pages cited like "pp. 992-994" or "pp. 1019-1049" are NOT impossible despite Vol 29A being a separate volume — they're in the continuous numbering.

**How to apply:** When seeing high page numbers for Brown AB John 29A (e.g., pp. 900-1200), do not flag as impossible. Volume 29A continuous pagination starts at p. 539. Add to "Multi-volume continuous-pagination convention" reference list alongside Marcus AYB 27A and Brown DM.
