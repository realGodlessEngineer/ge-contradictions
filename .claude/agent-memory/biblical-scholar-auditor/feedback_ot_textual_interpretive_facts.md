- **Samaritan Pentateuch Terah=145** (Gen 11:32, vs MT/LXX 205) is a REAL harmonizing variant (cf. Philo, *Migr.* 177) — don't flag as fabricated.
- **Hosea 11:5 emendation target**: the lo' relocates to the end of 11:4 (read לו), NOT to 10:15 — don't conflate with 10:15's "storm/cut off" content.
- **Thiele ENDORSED** the divided-kingdom reading of 2 Chr 16 — resist an auditor false-positive claiming "Thiele rejected it" (entry 276).
- **Seow's AB 18C dates Qohelet to the Persian period** (5th-4th c.), NOT Hellenistic/3rd c. — a drafting pass inverting this is a misinformation flag, not a legitimate alternate reading.
- **Sarna's *On the Book of Psalms* covers only** Pss 1, 8, 15, 19, 24, 30, 48, 82, 93, 94 (selected essays) — cites to any other psalm (e.g. Ps 89) from this work are fabricated.
- **Isaiah 8:2 has the longer form "Jeberechiah"**, not "Berechiah" as in Zech 1:1/Matt 23:35 — the two are not simply interchangeable.
- **KJV vs ESV Neh 7:70 versification differs**: KJV's governor gift = 530 garments; ESV = 30 garments + 500 minas silver — always audit a quoted figure against the SOURCE's own translation, not a different one.
- **Levitical-city lacuna is a conjectural emendation, not a textual variant**: the Josh 21/1 Chr 6 lost-Dan-heading theory is conjecture (`textual_variant_involved` should be false) — see Lee-Sak, *JBL* 136/4 (2017).
- **Andreasen's Queen Mother article** is *CBQ* 45 (1983) pp.179-194, not JBL (see also [[commentary-citation-facts]]).
- **Jael-Sisera seduction reading attribution**: proper scholars for the Judges 5 erotic-euphemism reading are Zakovitch/Niditch/Ackerman/Bal — not J. Glen Taylor.
- **Judith/Luke 1:42 intertextual attribution**: the Jael/Judith/Mary "blessed" intertextual reading belongs to Brittany Wilson (*CBQ* 2006), not Tina Pippin.
