William L. Lane, *The Gospel According to Mark*, NICNT (Eerdmans 1974) — the discussion of the Longer Ending of Mark (Mark 16:9-20) is in the **Supplementary Endings** section at approximately **pp. 601-611** (with the Freer Logion at pp. 606-611 specifically, and p. 602 covering codex evidence). The page p. 591 discusses Mark's ending at 16:8, not the Longer Ending content.

**Why:** Found in batch_25 entry 491 — AI draft cited Lane "pp. 591-595" for support of a Mark 16:17 ("in my name shall they cast out devils") discussion. That verse is part of the Longer Ending and is treated in the Supplementary Endings section ~601-611, not the body commentary on Mark 16:1-8.

**How to apply:** When citing Lane for Mark 16:9-20 (Longer Ending) material: use pp. 601-611. For commentary on Mark 16:1-8 (women at tomb): pp. 581-592 range. Total book is 678 pp.
