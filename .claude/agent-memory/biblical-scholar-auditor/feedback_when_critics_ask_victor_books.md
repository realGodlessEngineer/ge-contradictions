Norman L. Geisler and Thomas Howe, *When Critics Ask: A Popular Handbook on Bible Difficulties*, was originally published in 1992 by **Victor Books, Wheaton, Illinois** (ISBN 0-89693-698-8). Baker Books reprinted it in a later edition (ISBN 0-8010-1142-9), but the 1992 original is a Victor Books title.

**Pass-13 batch_18 finding**: Entries 342 and 345 both cited "Grand Rapids: Baker, 1992" — this conflates the original Victor 1992 publication with the Baker reprint imprint metadata. The correct 1992 citation is "Wheaton, IL: Victor Books, 1992."

**Why:** AI drafts default to Baker because the Baker reprint is more visible in current Christian-bookstore listings and Logos databases. The 1992 first edition imprint is Victor Books Wheaton.

**How to apply:** When auditing citations of Geisler-Howe *When Critics Ask* with year 1992, verify publisher is Victor Books Wheaton IL, not Baker Grand Rapids. This is a recurring pattern likely to appear in any batch citing this apologetics work alongside Archer's *Encyclopedia of Bible Difficulties* (which IS Zondervan Grand Rapids 1982).
