Bultmann, *The Gospel of John: A Commentary*, Philadelphia: Westminster, 1971 (also Oxford: Basil Blackwell, 1970 UK edition) — full translator credit is "G. R. Beasley-Murray, R. W. N. Hoare, and J. K. Riches" (Beasley-Murray as general editor).

**Why:** AI drafts frequently truncate to "G. R. Beasley-Murray" only, the lead/general editor, omitting Hoare and Riches. Verified via Brown 1972 review in SAGE Journals (JSTOR 10.1177/004057367202800420) and the official xiv+744 pp. Westminster edition title page.

**How to apply:** When auditing Bultmann John citations, always check that all three translator names appear. Same pattern as the Loeb Josephus Vol V co-translator rule (Thackeray AND Marcus, not Marcus alone). Caught at pass-11 of batch_24 after pass-9 and pass-10 returned 0; broke a 2-pass false plateau.
