When commentary mentions "Klein" as a critical scholar, identify the correct Klein volume by topic:
- 1 Samuel passages -> Ralph W. Klein, 1 Samuel, WBC 10, 2nd ed. (Thomas Nelson, 2000)
- 1 Chronicles passages -> Ralph W. Klein, 1 Chronicles, Hermeneia (Fortress, 2006)

**Why:** Klein wrote both. In batch_12 entry 236 (1 Samuel 15 / Amalekite herem), the commentary listed "McCarter, Hertzberg, Klein, Niditch" as critical scholars on 1 Samuel 15, but the scholarship list only had Klein 1 Chronicles Hermeneia (cited elsewhere in the batch for other entries). This was a parity gap requiring a Klein 1 Samuel WBC entry.

**How to apply:** When auditing scholarship parity, don't assume "Klein" mentioned elsewhere in the batch is the same Klein volume needed for the current entry. Match volume to passage scope.
