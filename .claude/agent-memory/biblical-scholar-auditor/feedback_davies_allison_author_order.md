The standard scholarly citation for the ICC Matthew commentary is **"Davies, W. D., and Dale C. Allison."** in that order. Davies was the senior author; Allison the junior collaborator who began work on Vol I and continued through Vols II and III. The Edinburgh ICC volumes always list Davies first on the title page.

**Why:** Batch_19 pass 2 entry 367 cited "Allison, Dale C., and W. D. Davies. A Critical and Exegetical Commentary on the Gospel According to Saint Matthew, Volume III." while every other entry in the same batch (entries 361, 371, 377, 380) correctly used Davies-first. AI drafts occasionally invert author order at one citation while preserving it at others.

**How to apply:** Sweep every Davies-Allison ICC Matthew citation for author order. The form is always "Davies, W. D., and Dale C. Allison." (or "Davies, W. D., and D. C. Allison."). Volume III's full subtitle is "Commentary on Matthew XIX-XXVIII" (the form already used in batch_19 entry 361).
