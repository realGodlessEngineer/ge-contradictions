Tsumura's NICOT *The Second Book of Samuel* (Eerdmans, 2019), ISBN 9780802870964, is **~400 pages** in print (publisher/Christianbook/Eerdmans listing: "Number of Pages: 400").

**Why:** An earlier version of this note claimed 320 pages and said to flag any citation >320. That figure was WRONG and would generate false positives. On 2026-06-11 (batch_00 commentary_fix audit) a Tsumura citation of "pp. 310-330" for 2 Sam 22 / Psalm 18 was checked against this note; verification against Christianbook (christianbook.com/.../9780802870964) and Eerdmans showed the book is 400 pp, so pp. 310-330 is in range and the citation is fine. Some retailers/Kindle list 401.

**How to apply:** Do NOT apply a 320-page cap to Tsumura 2 Samuel. Page ranges up to ~395 (body; allow margin for indices/back matter past ~390) are plausible. Only flag clearly impossible ranges (e.g., >400). The First Book of Samuel NICOT (2007, 698 pages) is a separate, larger volume. As always, verify a specific page range against the publisher before flagging on count alone — this note exists precisely because a remembered page total was stale.

Confirmed sources: Christianbook.com and Eerdmans.com product pages for ISBN 9780802870964 (both list 400 pages).
