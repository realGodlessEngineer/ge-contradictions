When commentary names a specific Barthelemy work for a textual-critical argument, verify which Barthelemy work actually contains that argument.

**Why:** Dominique Barthelemy authored multiple distinct textual-critical works:
- *Critique textuelle de l'Ancien Testament* (OBO 50/1-5, 1982-2015) — multi-volume comprehensive textual commentary covering the whole HB
- *The Story of David and Goliath: Textual and Literary Criticism* (OBO 73, 1986) — co-authored with Gooding, Lust, and Tov; specifically about the 1 Sam 17 textual problem

Barthelemy's MT-priority argument on David-Goliath/1 Sam 17 lives in the OBO 73 volume, not the Critique textuelle. AI drafts can conflate the two works because both are textual-critical Barthelemy outputs in the OBO series.

**How to apply:** When auditing commentary attributing an MT-priority/literary-criticism argument on 1 Samuel 16-18 to Barthelemy, check that the named work is the OBO 73 Story of David and Goliath volume, not the broader Critique textuelle multi-volume series. Scholarship lists may correctly cite OBO 73 while commentary names the wrong Barthelemy book — a soft parity mismatch easy to miss.
