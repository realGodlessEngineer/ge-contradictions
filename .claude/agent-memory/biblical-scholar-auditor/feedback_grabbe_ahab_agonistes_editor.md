name: Grabbe Ahab Agonistes is edited volume not single-author
description: Ahab Agonistes (LHBOTS 421) is an edited conference volume; cite Grabbe as "ed." not as author
type: feedback
---

Lester L. Grabbe is the **editor** of Ahab Agonistes: The Rise and Fall of the Omri Dynasty (LHBOTS 421, T&T Clark, 2007), not the sole author. The volume is the proceedings of the European Seminar in Historical Methodology and contains chapters by Knauf, Kottsieper, Lemaire, Na'aman, Niemann, Pruin, Thompson, Ussishkin, Warburton, and others. Grabbe wrote the introduction ("The Kingdom of Israel from Omri to the Fall of Samaria") and contributed analytical material.

**Why:** Pass-4 audit of batch_15 found "Lester L. Grabbe, Ahab Agonistes..." cited as if Grabbe were sole author (entry 285). When page ranges (e.g., pp. 28-32, 175-180) span multiple chapters in an edited volume, omitting "ed." misattributes work by other contributors to Grabbe.

**How to apply:** When citing Ahab Agonistes, write "Lester L. Grabbe, ed., Ahab Agonistes..." If a specific chapter is the source, cite the chapter author and title: e.g., "Lester L. Grabbe, 'The Kingdom of Israel from Omri to the Fall of Samaria,' in Ahab Agonistes, ed. Lester L. Grabbe, LHBOTS 421..." Apply same scrutiny to other Grabbe-edited European Seminar volumes (Like a Bird in a Cage, Good Kings and Bad Kings, etc.).
