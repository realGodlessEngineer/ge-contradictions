John 2:15 actual Greek is "τά τε πρόβατα καὶ τοὺς βόας" (ta te probata kai tous boas), not "te ta probata." AI inverts the te-ta clitic order.

**Why:** Batch 29 entry 579 had "*te ta probata kai tous boas*" — the postpositive enclitic *te* must follow the article *ta*, not precede it. Greek enclitic positioning requires "X te Y kai Z" pattern.

**How to apply:** Verify all transliterations of Greek phrases with the *te...kai* construction. The *te* always follows the first word of the first conjoined element. Same pattern applies to other postpositive particles (*men*, *de*, *gar*).
