id 428 ("Did John the Baptist recognize Jesus as the Son of God?" — John 1:29-34 baptismal
testimony vs. Matt 11:2-3/Luke 7:18-20 prison inquiry): discrepancy pole's named_skeptic is
D. F. Strauss, connective quotes him calling John's later doubt "psychologically impossible"
after his baptismal testimony. Confirmed REAL and near-verbatim — Strauss's *Life of Jesus
Critically Examined* (1846 Eng. tr., §46 "Was Jesus acknowledged by John as the Messiah? and
in what sense?", Ch. I "Relations Between Jesus and John the Baptist", §§44-48 pp. 209-234)
uses the exact phrase "psychologically impossible that he whose belief was originated or
confirmed by the baptismal sign ... should all at once have become unsteady in his conviction"
on p. 224 of the archive.org copy (item `lifeofjesuscriti00straiala`, 1902 1-vol Eliot tr.),
found via the fulltext search-inside API (see [[feedback_archive_org_fulltext_search_technique]]).

The reconcile pole's PD excerpt is Gill on Matthew 11:3 (GILL/40/11/3) — states John already
knew Jesus as Son of God/Lamb of God from the baptism, then supplies the disciples'-sake
harmonization; the excerpt is cleanly cut before Gill's own hedging ("Though some have thought
that John's faith was somewhat slackened...").
