Edwin R. Thiele (*The Mysterious Numbers of the Hebrew Kings*) ENDORSED the divided-kingdom / schism-era reading of 2 Chronicles 15:19 and 16:1: the "35th/36th year" counts years from the division of the kingdom after Solomon, not Asa's personal regnal years, placing the Baasha campaign in Asa's actual 16th year while Baasha still lived. Confirmed via Wikipedia (Mysterious Numbers) + multiple chronology sources.

**Why:** An earlier audit pass on entry 276 (When did Baasha die?) flagged HIGH-severity that the commentary "misattributed" the divided-kingdom reading to Thiele, claiming Thiele REJECTED it and treated the numbers as a corrupt gloss. That audit claim is FALSE — it inverts Thiele's actual position. The task brief explicitly carries a CRITICAL warning about exactly this false positive. The generator's commentary_out correctly resisted it (its change_summary documents the reversal).

**How to apply:** For 2 Chr 16:1 / Asa-Baasha entries: the divided-kingdom reading IS Thiele's (and Jamieson-Fausset-Brown's); the *copyist-error* reading (35/36 from original 15/16) is the Keil-Delitzsch / Geisler-Howe line. Do NOT flag a commentary for attributing the schism-era reading to Thiele — that is correct. Treat any prior-pass "Thiele rejected divided-kingdom dating" flag as itself the error.

**Publisher note:** the 1983 revised/3rd edition was **Zondervan** (Grand Rapids); Kregel is the later reprint house that currently sells it. Prefer Zondervan 1983 for the revised edition; Kregel 1983 is imprecise (low-severity at most). Related: [[when-critics-ask-victor-books]].
