David Aune's *Revelation* Word Biblical Commentary has **continuous pagination** across all three volumes:

- **WBC 52A** = Revelation 1-5 (Word Books, Dallas 1997): pp. 1-374
- **WBC 52B** = Revelation 6-16 (Thomas Nelson, Nashville 1998): pp. 377-903
- **WBC 52C** = Revelation 17-22 (Thomas Nelson, Nashville 1998): pp. 905-1354

**Why:** Batch_19 entry 367 cited "WBC 52C ... pp. 819-832" for Rev 14:10-11. But pp. 819-832 fall within 52B's range (Rev 6-16). The continuous pagination scheme makes it easy to assign correct page-range but wrong volume label. Rev 14 chapters live in 52B, Rev 20 in 52C.

**How to apply:** When citing a page from Aune Revelation, look up which volume the page-range falls in:
- pp. 1-374 → 52A (Dallas: Word Books, 1997)
- pp. 377-903 → 52B (Nashville: Thomas Nelson, 1998)
- pp. 905-1354 → 52C (Nashville: Thomas Nelson, 1998)

Note publisher difference: 52A is Word Books Dallas; 52B and 52C are Thomas Nelson Nashville. Both 52B and 52C are 1998.
