Luther's famous "right strawy epistle" (*eyn rechte stroern Epistel*) characterization of James appears in the 1522 Preface to the New Testament (in LW 35: 357-362), NOT in the Preface to the Epistles of St. James and St. Jude (in LW 35: 395-398).

**Why:** Encountered in batch_28 entry 542 where commentary text quoted the "strawy" phrase but scholarship cited only the James/Jude preface. The two prefaces are distinct works.

**How to apply:** When commentary text references Luther's "strawy epistle" comment about James, the scholarship citation must include the 1522 Preface to the NT (pp. 357-362). The James/Jude preface (pp. 395-398) contains other anti-James content but not the "strawy" line. Cite both for completeness.
