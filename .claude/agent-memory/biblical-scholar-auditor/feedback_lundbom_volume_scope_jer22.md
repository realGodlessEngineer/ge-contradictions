Lundbom's Anchor Bible Jeremiah:
- **AB 21A** = Jeremiah 1-20 (Doubleday 1999) ~934 pp
- **AB 21B** = Jeremiah 21-36 (Doubleday 2004) ~772 pp
- **AB 21C** = Jeremiah 37-52 (Doubleday 2004) 624 pp

So Jer 22 (Jeconiah curse) belongs to **AB 21B**, not 21A. Jer 52 belongs to 21C. Jer 1-20 alone belongs to 21A.

**Why:** Pass 5 of batch_16 caught entry 307 commentary saying "Lundbom in Anchor Bible Jeremiah 1-20/21A" while citing Jer 22:28-30. The scholarship entry correctly cited 21B; the commentary prose was inconsistent.

**How to apply:** When commentary cites Lundbom volume range, verify the volume corresponds to the actual Jeremiah passage being discussed. Cross-check commentary prose against scholarship list — if scholarship entry says 21B but commentary text names 21A, the commentary text needs correction.
