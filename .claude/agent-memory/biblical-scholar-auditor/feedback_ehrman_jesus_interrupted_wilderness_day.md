id 486 (Mark 1:12-13 forty-day wilderness temptation vs John 1:35-2:1 day-by-day
itinerary with no wilderness) cites Bart D. Ehrman, *Jesus, Interrupted* (HarperOne,
2009), pp. 40-41 as the named skeptic on the discrepancy pole. Verified via
archive.org fulltext of the book: pp. 40-41 carry the section header "Where Was
Jesus the Day After He Was Baptized?" which argues exactly this point — Synoptics
send Jesus to the wilderness immediately after baptism, but John's day-by-day
markers ("the next day" at 1:29, 1:35, 2:1) leave no room for it, closing with
"So where was Jesus the next day? It depends on which Gospel you read." The
connective's paraphrase ("leaves no room ... an invention the text never
signals") is a fair characterization of Ehrman's rhetorical point, not a
strawman — verdict: named_skeptic ok.

general Gospel-differences point) — same evidentiary bar as
