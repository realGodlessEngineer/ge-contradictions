Paine appears often as the discrepancy-pole named skeptic. Three outcome classes,
by whether the SPECIFIC argument attributed to him is actually his on THIS passage:

**(1) Right argument, wrong work title → connectives=flag only, named_skeptic=ok.**
id 33: the "changeable, passionate, vindictive Being... drowning the world...
repenting" passage IS genuine Paine, but lives in *Letters Concerning the Age of
Reason* (1797), "An Answer to a Friend"/"A Letter to a Christian Friend" (May 12,
1797) — NOT Age of Reason Part I (1794). When cited AS the Letters, the
attribution is correct (this note previously had it backwards; corrected
2026-06-23). id 163 (does God lie / 1 Kgs 22 lying spirit): "another blasphemous
story of God" is genuine Paine but belongs to the posthumous **"Biblical
Blasphemy"** essay (~1807/1818, associated with the Third Part), not Age of
Reason Part Second (1795) as first cited — remediated correctly on re-audit.

**(2) Right-enough work, argument NOT actually his on this passage → strawman +
connectives=flag.** Recurring especially on Gen/Deut "numerousness"-style
catalog tensions and general-character arguments mapped onto a specific verse
pair Paine never addresses:
- id 77 (many Jews? Gen 22/26 vs Deut 4 scattering) — no verifiable Paine
  argument on this pairing; REMEDIATED to Steve Wells/SAB (jews_many.html),
  now all-clean.
- id 145 (nonbelievers/Deut 13 stone-kin vs Lev 19 love) — Paine's real "tortures
  all her social ties" quote targets Numbers 31, not Deut 13; REMEDIATED to
  Wells/SAB (nonbelievers.html), now all-clean.
- id 181 (mamzer ten generations vs David/Judah-Tamar) — Paine's documented
  genealogy critique is Matthew-vs-Luke counts + Ruth/Boaz mockery, never the
  Judah-Tamar mamzer angle; still strawman as of last audit.
- id 202 (does God forgive? Josh 24:19 vs pardoning texts) — tektonics.org's own
  Paine index states he does NOT argue this; the tension is Wells/SAB's
  (jos/contra_list.html), not Paine's; still strawman as of last audit.
- id 131 (total vs incomplete conquest, Josh 15:63/Judg 1 Jebusites) — Paine
  DOES cite these verses but for LATE-AUTHORSHIP/dating, not the
  triumphalist-vs-realistic internal contradiction; REMEDIATED to Wells/SAB
  (driveout.html), now all-clean.

**(3) Right argument AND right work → all ok.** id 149 (God-is-love vs
God-hates-nations/Esau): Paine's Age of Reason genuinely contrasts a "cruel,
vindictive... more consistent to call it the word of a demon" Bible-God against
creation's "unchangeableness and eternal order, harmony, and goodness" — exactly
this tension. named_skeptic=ok, all clean, no strawman.

**Why:** Paine wrote broadly on Mosaic authorship, cruelty/vindictiveness, and
specific catalog atrocities (Numbers 31, 1 Kings 22, the "27 old bachelors"
genealogy joke, Ruth/Boaz), but the TRANSFORM pass sometimes maps his general
reputation as "the" Bible-contradiction skeptic onto a specific verse pairing he
never actually addressed.

**How to apply:** always verify the SPECIFIC argument (not just "Paine wrote
about this general topic") against primary Age of Reason / Letters / Biblical
Blasphemy text (ushistory.org, infidels.org, marxists.org, deism.com,
sacred-texts.com mirrors; tektonics.org/lp/painet02.php is a useful negative-check
index of what Paine does NOT argue). A remediation that swaps Paine for
Steve Wells/SAB when the SAB contra page genuinely lists the row's verse stack is
the reliable fix pattern seen repeatedly (ids 77, 131, 145).
