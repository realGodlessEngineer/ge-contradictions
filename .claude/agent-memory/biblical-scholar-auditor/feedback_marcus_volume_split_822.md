Marcus Mark two-volume division: **Vol 1 ends at 8:21; Vol 2 starts at 8:22 (Bethsaida blind man).**

- Vol 1: *Mark 1-8* (AB 27, Doubleday 2000) — Mark 1:1 to 8:21. Total ~568 pp.
- Vol 2: *Mark 8-16* (AYB 27A, Yale UP 2009) — Mark 8:22 to 16:8. Continuous pagination from p. 569 to ~1183.

**Why:** I made an auditor self-error in batch_27 pass 1 by "fixing" a Marcus AYB 27A cite for Mark 8:22-26 (Bethsaida blind man) to AB 27 Mark 1-8. That was wrong — the Bethsaida pericope is the OPENING pericope of Vol 2, not the closing pericope of Vol 1. Pass 2 caught the false fix via page-count check (p. 590-598 exceeds Vol 1's ~568 pp total).

**How to apply:**
- Mark 8:22-26 (Bethsaida blind man) = Vol 2 (AYB 27A).
- Mark 8:27-9:1 (Caesarea Philippi/Peter's confession) = Vol 2 (AYB 27A).
- Anything up through Mark 8:21 = Vol 1 (AB 27, Doubleday 2000).
- Continuous pagination >568 = AYB 27A by definition.
- Before "fixing" a Marcus volume attribution, check (a) the chapter:verse division at 8:21/8:22 and (b) the page number against the 568-page Vol 1 cap.
