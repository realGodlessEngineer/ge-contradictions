Joel Marcus, *Mark 8-16: A New Translation with Introduction and Commentary*, AYB 27A (Yale University Press, 2009).

**Pagination convention**: Continuous from Mark 1-8 (Doubleday 2000, xix + 569 pp). Content begins ~p. 569 and runs to ~p. 1183. The volume contains 672 total physical pages (60 roman prelim + 610 numbered continuous-arabic).

**Verification anchor**: Published scholarly citation "Marcus 2009, 594" for Mark 8:22-26 (Bethsaida blind man) — the first commentary section in AYB 27A. Mark 8:22 commentary begins ~p. 592.

**Approximate pericope map** for AYB 27A:
- Mark 8:22-26 Bethsaida blind: ~pp. 592-602
- Mark 8:27-9:1: ~pp. 603-625
- Mark 9-13 commentary: ~pp. 625-950
- Mark 14:30 / 14:66-72 Peter denial: ~pp. 998-1015
- Mark 15:21-39 crucifixion: ~pp. 1037-1068
- Mark 15:37-38 veil: ~pp. 1056-1068
- Mark 16:1-8 empty tomb: ~pp. 1078-1088
- Mark 16:9-20 longer ending appendix: ~pp. 1088-1104

**Why:** Brill review (Horizons in Biblical Theology 32.1, 2010) describes the volume as "Pp. lx + 610" which can mislead an auditor into thinking pagination resets at 1. Yale's catalog page count (672) does NOT reflect 1-672 range — it reflects 60 + 610 = 670 ≈ 672 pages of content, but the arabic numbering is continuous from Mark 1-8 not 1-610. AB/AYB series convention is continuous pagination for multi-volume sets.

**How to apply:** Citations to Marcus AYB 27A above p. 700 are NOT impossible — they are within the continuous-pagination range. Do not flag pp. 998, 1056, 1078, 1088 as out-of-range. Compare instead to the approximate pericope map above.
