name: Merrill Chronicles NAC hallucination pattern
description: Eugene Merrill is frequently miscited as the NAC volume author for 1-2 Chronicles; he is not
type: feedback
---

When AI-drafted scholarship cites "Eugene H. Merrill, 1, 2 Chronicles, New American Commentary 6 (Nashville: B&H)" — this is a HALLUCINATION. Correct citations:

- Merrill's NAC volume is **Deuteronomy** (NAC 4, Broadman & Holman, 1994), NOT Chronicles
- The NAC Chronicles volume is **J. A. Thompson, 1, 2 Chronicles, NAC 9** (Broadman & Holman, 1994)
- Merrill DID publish a Chronicles commentary in 2015, but in the **Kregel Exegetical Library** (Grand Rapids: Kregel Academic, 2015), not NAC

**Why:** Merrill is a well-known conservative Old Testament scholar associated with NAC (for Deuteronomy) and with multiple OT commentaries. AI drafts conflate his NAC association with his 2015 Chronicles commentary and produce the wrong series/publisher.

**How to apply:** Whenever you see "Eugene Merrill" + "1, 2 Chronicles" + "NAC" + any volume number, treat as a hallucination and replace with the Kregel Exegetical Library 2015 citation. Also check commentary prose for "Merrill in the NAC volume on Chronicles" — that phrasing is wrong; substitute "Merrill in his Kregel Chronicles commentary" or similar. The NAC 6 slot is Cabal's *1, 2 Kings* (or similar — NAC numbering: 4=Deut, 5=Joshua, 6=Judges-Ruth(Block), 7=1-2 Sam, 8=1-2 Kings, 9=1-2 Chr).
