The Heth-Wenham *Jesus and Divorce* book has two distinct editions with different subtitles paired with different publishers:

- **UK 1984 first edition**: Hodder & Stoughton, London. Subtitle = "*Towards an Evangelical Understanding of New Testament Teaching*"
- **US 1985 edition**: Thomas Nelson, Nashville. Subtitle = "*The Problem with the Evangelical Consensus*"

**Why:** AI scholar enrichment frequently pairs the US subtitle with the UK publisher (or vice versa). The US "Problem" subtitle is more recognizable from Wenham's later self-citations and from US evangelical reception literature, while the UK "Towards" subtitle reflects the original UK marketing framing for the same content.

**How to apply:** When you see Hodder & Stoughton 1984 cited, the subtitle must be "Towards an Evangelical Understanding of New Testament Teaching." When you see Thomas Nelson 1985 cited, the subtitle must be "The Problem with the Evangelical Consensus." A 2010 Wipf & Stock reprint exists of the US edition.
