Verified real (id 331, "How many of Asaph's offspring returned from Babylon?", Ezra 2:41=128
vs Neh 7:44=148): John Haley, *An Examination of the Alleged Discrepancies of the Bible*
(1874), discusses the Arah head-count variant (Ezra 2:5 / Neh 7:10, 775 vs 652) on p.396,
then states on p.397: "The other cases, some twenty in number, which appear from a comparison
of Ezra ii. 6-60, with Neh. vii. 11-67, are to be explained in the same manner" (i.e. as
copyists' blunders in the numerals). Confirmed verbatim via archive.org identifier
`examinationof00hale`, full-text search endpoint
`https://ia800706.us.archive.org/fulltext/inside.php?item_id=examinationof00hale&doc=examinationof00hale&path=/16/items/examinationof00hale&q=...`
(OCR renders "6-60" as "6-90"/"6-Qo" — garbled scan, not a real variant reading; the range
Ezra 2:6-60 is otherwise unambiguous and Neh 7:11-67 confirms it).

**Why:** this generalizing sentence is Haley's *only* explicit treatment of the whole family
of Ezra-2/Nehemiah-7 numeric-registry gaps (Arah, Asaph's singers, and ~18 other entries) — he
never names each family individually (no "Asaph" or "singers" hit anywhere in the book's
full-text index). A `deeper_learning.defense.pd_work` note citing this sentence for ANY
Ezra-2-vs-Neh-7 headcount contradiction in this corpus (not just Asaph) is legitimate and
on-topic, since the cited range (2:6-60) covers virtually every family in the list.

**How to apply:** if a future work file for another Ezra-2/Neh-7 family-count contradiction
(e.g. Bethlehem, Ater, Hashum, etc. — same registry, different verse pair) cites this same
Haley quote as its pd_work, treat it as pre-verified real/on-topic — no need to re-run the
archive.org search. Also: the SAB page `contra/asaph.html` on skepticsannotatedbible.com is
confirmed live and states the 128-vs-148 Asaph gap directly (Steve Wells, named_skeptic=ok).
