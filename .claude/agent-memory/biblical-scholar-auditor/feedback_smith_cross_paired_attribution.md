name: Smith and Cross paired-attribution parity rule
description: When commentary pairs Mark Smith and Frank Cross as joint scholarly authority on early Israelite anthropomorphism, both need scholarship entries
type: feedback
---

When commentary in the contradictions DB attributes a specific scholarly thesis to a name-pair like "Mark Smith and Frank Cross have both argued...", both scholars require scholarship-array parity entries. The natural Cross citation for divine anthropomorphism / early Israelite religion theses is *Canaanite Myth and Hebrew Epic* (Harvard 1973), pp. 145-194 (chapters on Yahweh as warrior god and the divine council).

**Why:** Discovered in batch_18 entry 354 (Does God sleep?) pass 2, where Smith was cited via *Early History of God* but Cross was named in equal authority in the commentary without scholarship parity. The passing-mention parity rule applies even when a scholar appears as half of a two-name authority cluster — a named scholar carrying argumentative weight needs an entry. Distinct from generic apologist-list mentions ("Carson, Schreiner, Moo") which can remain unentried.

**How to apply:** When auditing a commentary, scan for two-name authority pairings ("Smith and Cross", "Dunn and Wright", "Crenshaw and Murphy"). If both names carry equal argumentative weight in the prose and only one is in the scholarship array, add a parity entry for the other using the work most relevant to the specific thesis being attributed. Avoid manufactured parity when the scholar truly is in passing/list framing.
