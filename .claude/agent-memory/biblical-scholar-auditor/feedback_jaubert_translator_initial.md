The translator of Annie Jaubert's *The Date of the Last Supper* (Alba House, 1965) is consistently cited in scholarly bibliographies as **I. Rafferty** (initial only). The expansion to "Isaac Rafferty" appears in no verifiable source.

**Why:** Found in batch_25 entry 493 — AI draft expanded "I. Rafferty" to "Isaac Rafferty" without warrant. Scholars who cite Jaubert (e.g., the 2015 Verbum Vitae article on Last Supper chronology) preserve the initial form.

**How to apply:** For Jaubert's English translation, always cite "Translated by I. Rafferty." Do not expand the initial. Pattern likely applies to other older Alba House / Catholic Biblical Quarterly Monograph Series translations where translator first names are not in the title page or library cataloging.
