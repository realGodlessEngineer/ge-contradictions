Dunn's *The Epistle to the Galatians* in Black's New Testament Commentary (1993) has two editions:
- UK original: London: A&C Black, 1993
- US: Peabody, MA: Hendrickson, 1993

The compound "London: Hendrickson" is wrong — Hendrickson is based in Peabody, MA, not London.

**Why:** Encountered in batch_28 entry 555 with "London: Hendrickson, 1993" — publisher/city mismatch.

**How to apply:** When citing BNTC commentaries that have both UK and US editions, choose one city/publisher pair consistently. Default to "Peabody: Hendrickson" for US distribution or "London: A&C Black" for UK original.
