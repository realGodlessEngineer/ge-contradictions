Thrall's ICC commentary on II Corinthians has two volumes with discontinuous pagination:
- Vol 1 (1994): II Cor 1-7, xxxvi + 501 pp.
- Vol 2 (2000): II Cor 8-13, xvi + 475 pp.

Vol 2 starts at p. 1 after roman-numeraled front matter; it does NOT continue from p. 502.

**Pass-9 batch_18 entry 357 case**: Citation for 2 Cor 10:5 commentary gave "Thrall ... Vol. 2 ... pp. 612-625" — impossible, because Vol II only has 475 pages. The hallucinated range looked plausible if one assumed continuous pagination (501 + 111 = 612), but that assumption is false.

**Why:** AI drafts confuse two-volume ICC pagination conventions. Some ICC sets (e.g., Davies-Allison Matthew 3-vol) are also independently paginated, but the AI defaults to continuous-style numbering plausibility. Vol 2 of any 2-volume work should NEVER exceed its own published page count.

**How to apply:** Before accepting any page citation for a multi-volume work, check the published total page count of THAT specific volume. If the citation exceeds the volume's last page, it is hallucinated. For Thrall ICC Vol 2 specifically, cap at p. 475. When the verse-location is known but specific page cannot be verified, switch to "ad loc. [verse]" rather than inventing a page.
