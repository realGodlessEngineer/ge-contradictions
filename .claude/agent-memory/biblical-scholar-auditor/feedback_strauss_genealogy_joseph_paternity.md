id 390 ("Was Joseph the father of Jesus?"): the discrepancy pole's named_skeptic is D. F. Strauss,
connective "the genealogies exist to prove Davidic descent through Joseph, so denying his paternity
nullifies that very claim." Confirmed REAL and fairly represented — Strauss's *Life of Jesus
Critically Examined* (English tr. 1846; German original *Das Leben Jesu*, 1835) discusses this exact
tension in §27 ("Retrospect of the genealogies"), arguing "if Jesus was not the son of Joseph, the
genealogy is worthless" as evidence of Davidic lineage. Full text at Project Gutenberg
(gutenberg.org/files/64037/64037-h/64037-h.htm).

The reconcile pole's PD excerpt is Gill on Acts 2:30 (GILL/44/2/30), reading "the fruit of his loins
... according to the flesh" as fulfilled through Mary's own Davidic lineage rather than Joseph's
paternity — a legitimate, verbatim match with a non-distorting trailing "…" truncation.

deeper_learning pd_work (John Haley, *An Examination of the Alleged Discrepancies of the Bible*, 1874)
verified VERBATIM via archive.org fulltext search-inside (see
[[feedback_archive_org_fulltext_search_technique]]): item `examinationof00hale`, p. 342 — "If Mary was
the daughter of Heli, then Jesus was strictly a descendant of David, not only legally, through his
reputed father, but actually, by direct personal descent, through his mother." Exact match to the
note's paraphrase.

deeper_learning link (gotquestions.org/Mary-lineage.html) confirmed real, on-topic, allowlisted —
argues Luke 3:23-38 records Mary's descent through Heli.
