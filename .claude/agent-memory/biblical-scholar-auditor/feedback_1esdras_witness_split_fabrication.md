When commentary on Ezra 2 / Nehemiah 7 family rolls cites 1 Esdras 5 as "a third witness giving still another number" or "X in some witnesses," the specific claim must be verified against the actual 1 Esdras 5 text. AI drafts repeatedly fabricate three-way splits where 1 Esdras actually agrees with either Ezra or Nehemiah.

**Confirmed verifiable readings (RV1895 main + footnote variants), verified via ebible.org/eng-rv/1ES05.htm:**
- 1 Esd 5:13 (Bani): 648 (agrees with Neh 7:15 against Ezra 2:10 = 642)
- 1 Esd 5:13 (Bebai): 623 (agrees with Ezra 2:11 against Neh 7:16 = 628)
- 1 Esd 5:13 (Astad/Azgad): 1,322 main, 3,222 or 3,622 variants (Ezra 1,222 / Neh 2,322) — main reading is CLOSE to Ezra, NOT 3,222
- 1 Esd 5:14 (Adonikam): 667 (agrees with Neh against Ezra 666)
- 1 Esd 5:14 (Bagoi/Bigvai): 2,066 (Ezra 2,056 / Neh 2,067) — close to Neh
- 1 Esd 5:14 (Adinu/Adin): 454 (agrees with Ezra against Neh 655) — NOT a "third number"
- 1 Esd 5:17 (Bethlehem/Bethlomon): 123; 5:18 Netophas: 55 — combined 178 (third number; Ezra 179, Neh 188)
- 1 Esd 5:22 (Calamolalus and Onus / Lod-Hadid-Ono): 725 (agrees with Ezra against Neh 721)
- 1 Esd 5:23 (Sanaas/Senaah): 3,330 MAIN reading (third number; Ezra 3,630 / Neh 3,930) — NOT hedged "in some witnesses"
- 1 Esd 5:27 (Asaph singers): 128 main, variant 148 — NOT 200
- 1 Esd 5:42 (singing men and women): 245 MAIN reading (agrees with Neh against Ezra 200) — NOT a "third figure in some witnesses"

**Why:** Batch_17 pass 2 found two fabricated 1 Esdras claims: Adin "still another number in some witnesses" (actually agrees with Ezra) and Asaph "200 in some witnesses" (no such variant attested). Batch_17 pass 3 found three additional pattern-instances: (1) Azgad commentary stated 1 Esd 5:13 "reads 3,222" when main reading is 1,322; (2) singing men/women entry 334 claimed 245 was a "third figure in some witnesses" when it's the main reading agreeing with Nehemiah; (3) Bebai entry 322 invoked "three witnesses three counts" framing in a chapter where 1 Esd agrees with Ezra (two-against-one, not three-way). Batch_17 pass 4 found one additional instance: (4) Bethlehem/Netophah entry 328 claimed "the Greek text in some witnesses has yet another total" when 1 Esd 5:17-18 main reading sums to 178 (a real third number, but stated as marginal-variant rather than main reading). Batch_17 pass 5 found one rhetorical-overreach instance: (5) entry 321 stated 1 Esd 5 "gives yet a third set of numbers for most of these families" when verified count shows third numbers appear in only ~4-5 of 11 attested entries (less than half). This is a generalized-framing overreach rather than a specific-number fabrication, but still falls under the same pattern.

**How to apply:** For any commentary citing 1 Esdras 5 against Ezra 2 / Neh 7 family rolls, look up the specific verse in RV1895 (ebible.org/eng-rv/1ES05.htm) or NRSA. If commentary claims a "third number," verify it actually is a third number (not an agreement with one Hebrew witness). If 1 Esdras actually agrees with one of the Hebrew witnesses, the argument is "two-against-one" not "three-way disagreement" and the commentary must be rewritten accordingly. Pay special attention: AI drafts conflate variants and main-text readings to construct rhetorically convenient "three witnesses" arguments.
