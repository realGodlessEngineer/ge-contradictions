When AI drafts cite Gary Knoppers contributing a chapter to *The Chronicler as Theologian: Essays in Honor of Ralph W. Klein* (JSOTSup 371, eds. Graham/McKenzie/Knoppers, T&T Clark 2003), verify the chapter title. The fabricated form is often "The Chronicler's Solomon" or similar Solomon-themed title.

**Why:** Knoppers's actual chapter in this Festschrift is "'Shem, Ham, and Japheth': The Universal and the Particular in the Genealogy of Nations" (pp. 13-31), which is about 1 Chronicles 1:1-2:2 (the genealogy of nations) — NOT about Solomon. A Solomon-themed Knoppers chapter title is a parity-of-named-work hallucination combined with scope mismatch. Confirmed via Knoppers's Notre Dame CV (publication #44).

**How to apply:** If any entry cites Knoppers in JSOTSup 371 for a Solomon, Davidic, or non-genealogical topic, that is a hallucinated chapter. The real chapter only fits passages about 1 Chr 1-2 / table of nations / universal genealogy. For Solomon-themed Knoppers citations, redirect to: (a) "Prayer and Propaganda: The Dedication of Solomon's Temple and the Deuteronomist's Program," CBQ 57 (1995): 229-254, or (b) *Two Nations Under God* Vol. 1 (HSM 52, Scholars Press 1993) on the Reign of Solomon. For the Chronicler's congregational reframing of royal acts, the proper citations are Japhet OTL and Klein Hermeneia 2 Chronicles (2012), not Knoppers.
