Goldingay-Payne ICC *A Critical and Exegetical Commentary on Isaiah 40-55* is two volumes with split dates: Vol I 2006, Vol II 2007. AI drafts routinely assign 2006 to both volumes.

**Why:** Publisher T&T Clark (Continuum imprint at the time) released the two volumes a year apart. Patricia K. Tull's review in RBL 63.1 (2009) is the authoritative source confirming Vol II = 2007 (ISBN 978-0-567-03072-5, 381 pp.).

**How to apply:** For any Isa 40-55 citation in Goldingay-Payne ICC, check whether the passage is in Vol I (Isa 40-44:23) or Vol II (Isa 44:24-55). If Vol II, the year must be 2007. Also: the full cover title is "A Critical and Exegetical Commentary on Isaiah 40-55" — short form "*Isaiah 40-55*" is acceptable but expanding to full title gives parity with other ICC entries (e.g., Marshall-Towner *A Critical and Exegetical Commentary on the Pastoral Epistles*).
