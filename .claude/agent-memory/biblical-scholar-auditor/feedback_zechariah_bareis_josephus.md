When commentary references Zechariah son of Bareis/Baris/Baruch from Josephus's *Jewish War* in connection with Matt 23:35, verify the role precisely.

**Why:** Pass-9 audit on batch_16 entry 315 (commentary on Matthew 23:35) had it inverted: it called Bareis "a Zealot leader killed in the temple." Per Josephus, *War* 4.335-344, Zechariah son of Baris was an eminent citizen — explicitly opposed to the Zealots — whom they murdered after a mock trial in the temple courts (winter 67-68 CE).

**How to apply:** Whenever AI drafts mention "Zechariah son of Bareis" (or Baris/Baruch) in relation to Matthew 23:35 commentary:
- Verify he is described as the *victim* killed by Zealots, not a Zealot leader.
- The standard scholarly attribution (Davies-Allison ICC, Brown DOM) treats this as a possible source of Matthew's patronymic confusion.
- Cite as: Josephus, *War* 4.335-344 (or "4.5.4" in older numbering).
- Date: c. 67-68 CE during the Jewish War.

This is a recurring AI-drafted misidentification: the Greek transliteration of "Baris" can be confused with the surrounding zealot context, leading to inverted descriptions.
