Id 408 (Deut 4:2 "don't add to the law" vs Mt 5:21-22 "but I say to you" on
murder/anger). The named skeptic (Joel Marcus, "The Enigma of the Antitheses,"
*New Testament Studies* 69.2 [2023]: 121-37) is a REAL article by a real Duke
NT scholar — but its own abstract explicitly splits the six Matthean antitheses
into two groups with OPPOSITE conclusions:
- Antitheses 1-2 (5:21-30, murder/adultery — includes THIS contradiction's verse):
  "easy to interpret ... as intensifications of the Mosaic law." Marcus does NOT
  read this one as revision.
- Antitheses 3-5 (5:31-48, divorce/oaths/retaliation): the hard cases where Marcus
  argues the Matthean Jesus is "not seconding Sinai but correcting it" under his
  own eschatological/messianic authority — genuine revision of positive law.

A connective/skeptic-summary that says "Marcus argues Matthew's 'but I say' formula
lets Jesus revise Sinai under his own authority" and attaches this to the murder/anger
antithesis (5:21-22) inverts Marcus's own explicit position on that specific verse —
he'd side with the RECONCILE pole there, not discrepancy. This is a strawman even
though the person, article, journal, volume/issue, and year are all real and correctly
cited — verify not just "is this article real" but "does this article's conclusion
on THIS specific sub-passage match what the connective claims," especially for
multi-part texts (antitheses, plague narratives, genealogies) where scholars often
differentiate verse-by-verse or unit-by-unit.

**Why:** extends [[sonnet-named-skeptic-fabrication]] — a citation can be 100% real
and still be a strawman if the source differentiates by sub-passage and the summary
picks the wrong sub-passage's conclusion. Abstract-level verification (WebSearch)
was sufficient here since Marcus states the split plainly in the abstract itself.

**How to apply:** when a named-skeptic connective cites a scholar's article covering
a MULTI-PART passage (antitheses, six days of creation, genealogies, plague cycle),
check the abstract/summary for internal differentiation before accepting the citation
as support for one specific member of that set. Flagged: named_skeptic=strawman,
connectives=flag, discrepancy_integrity=relabel_missing (no real skeptic in substance
presses this exact sub-passage's tension even though the cited work is genuine).
