**Implication:** Acts 21:27-28:31 (trial narratives including Acts 22, 26) must be in pp. 231-242 if treated. Citations of Acts 22 or Acts 26 in Conzelmann at "pp. 187-188" or any pre-p. 230 location are impossible.

**Why:** Encountered in batch_28 entry 541 where Conzelmann pp. 187-188 was cited for Acts 26 — out of range.

**How to apply:** Use this page-cap to validate any Conzelmann Acts citation; reject citations that place trial narratives (Acts 21:27+) in the pre-230 range.
