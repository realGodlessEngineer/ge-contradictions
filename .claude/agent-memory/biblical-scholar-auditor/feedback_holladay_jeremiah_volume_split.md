Holladay's *Jeremiah* commentary in Hermeneia is two volumes:
- **Vol 1: Chapters 1-25**, Fortress 1986 (xxii + 682 pp)
- **Vol 2: Chapters 26-52**, Fortress 1989 (xxxi + 543 pp)

When citing Holladay for any Jeremiah passage, the chapter must match the volume:
- Jer 1-25 → Vol 1 (1986)
- Jer 26-52 → Vol 2 (1989) — includes Jer 34 (Zedekiah's fate), Jer 36 (scroll burning), Jer 39 (fall of Jerusalem), etc.

**Why:** Pass-2 audit of batch_15 entry 298 found Holladay's *Jeremiah 1...Chapters 1-25* (1986) cited for Jer 36:30 — but Jer 36 is in Vol 2 (1989). The volume number, subtitle, and year all needed correction.

**How to apply:** Check chapter scope vs volume number/year. The Hermeneia volume year discriminates: 1986 = Vol 1 (chs 1-25), 1989 = Vol 2 (chs 26-52). If a passage in chs 26-52 is paired with "1986" date or "Chapters 1-25" subtitle, that's a volume miscitation.
