Westcott's "The Gospel According to St. John" originally published in Speaker's Commentary (ed. F. C. Cook) in 1880, then separately by John Murray in 1882. Greek-text edition (with Westcott-Hort foundation) published posthumously by John Murray in 1908 in two volumes.

**Why:** AI often defaults to "Eerdmans" or "Macmillan" for older British commentaries; Westcott's John is Murray. Year 1881 is wrong (1882 is the separate-publication year; 1880 is the Speaker's Commentary year).

**How to apply:** When citing Westcott's John commentary for John 7:39 or other passages with the "two givings of the Spirit" distinction (which the entry-492 commentary attributes to Westcott as predecessor to D. A. Carson PNTC), use "John Murray, 1882" for the single-volume commentary or "John Murray, 1908" for the two-volume Greek-text edition.
