Jon D. Levenson's Sinai and Zion: An Entry into the Jewish Bible has two distinct editions that AI drafts routinely conflate:

- 1985 first edition: New Voices in Biblical Studies series. Publisher: Winston Press, Minneapolis (xi + 227 pp.). ISBN 0-86683-961-5. Also marketed as "A Seabury Book."
- 1987 paperback reprint: Harper & Row, San Francisco.

**Pass-12 batch_18 entry 344 error case**: Citation gave "New Voices in Biblical Studies. San Francisco: Harper & Row, 1985" — this mixes the 1987 Harper city with the 1985 Winston year. Pages 89-184 also extended past Chapter 2 (Zion) end at p. 178 in the Winston edition.

**Chapter pagination in 1985 Winston edition (227 pp.)**:
- Chapter 1 (Sinai: The Mountain of the Covenant): pp. 15-86
- Chapter 2 (Zion: The Mountain of the Temple): pp. 89-178 (some sources show pp. 87-177)
- Chapter 3 (The Manifold Relationships between Sinai and Zion): pp. 187-209

**Why:** The New Voices in Biblical Studies series was published by Winston Press Minneapolis (later Seabury). AI drafting confuses this with the later Harper paperback because Harper's name is more recognizable in NT-studies citations.

**How to apply:** When auditing Levenson Sinai and Zion citations, verify publisher matches edition year. 1985 = Winston Minneapolis; 1987 = Harper & Row San Francisco. Page ranges should match the 1985 edition pagination (Chapter 2 ends at p. 178 not p. 184).
