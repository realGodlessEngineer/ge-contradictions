For the intertextual reading that Luke 1:42 deliberately echoes the "blessed among women" formula from Judges 5:24 (Jael) and Judith 13:18 (Judith), the canonical scholarly reference is:

Wilson, Brittany E. "Pugnacious Precursors and the Bearer of Peace: Jael, Judith, and Mary in Luke 1:42." *Catholic Biblical Quarterly* 68 (2006): 436-456.

**Why:** AI-drafted commentary frequently attributes this reading to Tina Pippin (Agnes Scott College, feminist apocalyptic studies — primarily Revelation, not Luke 1). Pippin has not published the signature work on this Luke/Judith intertextual case. Wilson's 2006 CBQ article is the standard reference and is widely cited.

**How to apply:** When commentary text attributes the Luke/Judith/Jael "blessed among women" intertextual reading to a specific scholar, verify that the scholar named has actually published on this topic. Default-suspect Tina Pippin attributions for this argument; substitute Brittany Wilson (CBQ 2006) or general Lukan scholarship (Brown, Fitzmyer themselves note the parallel).
