Brown DM (Doubleday 1994) is two-volume continuous pagination: Vol 1 = pp. 1-879 (xxviii + 879); Vol 2 = pp. 880-1608. Any citation of "Vol. 2" with a page in the 1-879 range is automatically wrong — that page lives in Vol 1. Yale 2010 reprint says Vol 1 is 912pp total (with apparatus); content pagination remains same.

**Why:** Pass 2 of batch_24 caught entry 472 citing "ABRL. Vol. 2. ... pp. 845-848" for the Mark 15:25 / John 19:14 hour discrepancy. pp. 845-848 is mid-§31 Pilate trial in Vol 1, not Vol 2; the actual hour-discrepancy material is Vol 2 §40 around pp. 957-959. Confirmed Vol 1/Vol 2 boundary pattern flagged by pass 1.

**How to apply:** For any Brown DM citation, instantly check the page number against the 877/879 boundary. Pages 1-877 = Vol 1; pages 879-1608 = Vol 2. If "Vol. 2 ... pp. <879" appears, it is either wrong-volume tag or wrong-page-range hallucination. Common error zone is pp. 845-877 (late Vol 1 Pilate-trial material that AI conflates with crucifixion timing in Vol 2). Content-volume map: Vol 1 = Gethsemane / Jewish trial / Roman trial through mocking (through §32, ending ~p. 877). Vol 2 = crucifixion (§§37-44) / burial (§§45-47) / resurrection prelude.
