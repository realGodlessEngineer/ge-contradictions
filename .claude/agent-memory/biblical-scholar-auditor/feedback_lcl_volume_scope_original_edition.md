LCL 433 (Loeb Classical Library 433), Josephus *Jewish Antiquities* Vol IX, was published in 1965 with Louis H. Feldman as translator. The **original 1965 volume contained Books XVIII, XIX, and XX** (combined as Vol IX, 813 pp.).

Later, the Loeb Library re-paginated and renumbered: the current **Vol VIII = Books 18-19**, and **Vol X = Book 20 + general index**. The LCL series number 433 remained attached to the 18-19 portion.

**Why:** AI drafts citing LCL 433 1965 may say "Books XVIII-XIX" when the citation actually references material from Book 20 (e.g., Ant. 20.197-203 about Annas's sons being high priests). The 1965 publication is one bound volume covering XVIII-XX; citing it as XVIII-XIX is wrong if the work cites Book 20 material.

**How to apply:** If a citation to LCL 433 1965 includes section references in Book 20 (e.g., 20.197 for Annas's sons; 20.198-203 for Caiaphas family), the scope must be "Books XVIII-XX". If only Book 18-19 sections are cited, "Books XVIII-XIX" is acceptable for the current re-paginated Vol VIII edition. Caught in batch 23 entry 459. Same pattern likely applies to other LCL series where original-edition volume scope was later split.
