Josephus *Antiquities* 8.249 (verified at lexundria.com/j_aj and Whiston) describes Rehoboam marrying "another of his own kindred ... a daughter of Absalom by Tamar, whose name was Maachah," and naming their son Abijah his successor.

**What Josephus DOES say:** Maachah is Absalom's *daughter* (by a woman Tamar); Abijah's mother is Maachah.

**What Josephus does NOT say:** he never mentions **Uriel of Gibeah**, and he does NOT attest the modern "granddaughter" reconstruction (Tamar = Absalom's daughter who married Uriel and bore Maachah). The Tamar–Uriel *marriage* is a Keil-Delitzsch/Archer harmonizing inference stitched from 2 Sam 14:27 + 2 Chr 13:2, found nowhere in Josephus.

**Why:** A common AI hallucination in this batch's draft was citing Josephus 8.249-250 as ancient corroboration of the Tamar-Uriel-Maachah link. That is fabricated. But equally, do NOT over-correct: Josephus genuinely connects Maachah to Absalom and a Tamar — just not via Uriel and not as a granddaughter.

**How to apply:** For entry 273 (Abijam's mother, 1 Kgs 15:2 Maachah/Abishalom vs 2 Chr 13:2 Michaiah/Uriel): a commentary_out that says "Josephus transmits no Tamar-Uriel-Maachah link" is CORRECT and clean. Flag only if a draft re-asserts Josephus as evidence FOR the Uriel marriage. The granddaughter bridge is grammatically possible (*bat* can skip a generation, cf. Gen 29:5 Laban "son of Nahor") but evidentially unsecured — "partially_harmonized" is the defensible label. Related: [[josephus-harmonization-misattribution]].
