In commentary connecting the prophet Zechariah (Zech 1:1 "son of Berechiah son of Iddo") to Isaiah 8:2's witness "Zechariah son of Jeberechiah," AI drafts often state the names as identical when they are not.

- Isaiah 8:2 (KJV/Hebrew יְבֶרֶכְיָהוּ): "Jeberechiah" — longer theophoric form ending -yahu
- Zech 1:1 / Matt 23:35 (Hebrew בֶּרֶכְיָה / Greek Βαραχίας): "Berechiah/Barachias" — shorter form

Both names share the same root (brk + Yahweh = "Yah blesses") and scholars treat them as variant theophoric forms, but they are not strictly identical spellings. Commentary that uses one form when the source has the other should either flag the variation or use the form actually present in each source.

**Why:** Batch_17 entry 336 (Zechariah Iddo's son or grandson) commentary stated "Zechariah son of Berechiah is also mentioned in Isaiah 8:2" when the actual Isaiah 8:2 form is "Jeberechiah" — a minor but verifiable inaccuracy.

**How to apply:** For any Zechariah-genealogy commentary touching Zech 1:1 / Isaiah 8:2 / Matt 23:35 / 2 Chr 24:20-22, verify each source's exact name form. The connection between Isaiah 8:2's Jeberechiah and Zech 1:1's Berechiah is real but should be expressed as "longer theophoric variant" rather than as identity.
