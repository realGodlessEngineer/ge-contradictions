The Expositor's Bible Commentary has TWO editions with different volume numbering for Matthew:

- **Original EBC (1984)**: 12-volume set, edited by Frank E. Gaebelein. Matthew is in **Vol 8** (Matthew, Mark, Luke), Carson authored Matthew section.
- **Revised EBC (2010)**: 13-volume set, edited by Tremper Longman III and David E. Garland. Matthew is in **Vol 9** (Matthew–Mark), Carson + Walter Wessel + Mark Strauss.

**Why:** AI scholar enrichment frequently combines "Rev. ed., Zondervan, 2010" with "EBC Vol 8" — that's the Revised Edition publication year paired with the Original Edition volume number. Internally inconsistent.

**How to apply:** If "Rev. ed." or "2010" is in the citation, the volume number must be 9. If "1984" or no edition specified for the older 12-volume set, volume can be 8. Standard form for the 2010 chapter cite: `Carson, D. A. Matthew. In The Expositor's Bible Commentary, Revised Edition. Vol. 9: Matthew–Mark. Edited by Tremper Longman III and David E. Garland. Grand Rapids: Zondervan, 2010, [pp.].`
