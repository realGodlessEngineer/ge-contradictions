The well-known textual emendation of Hosea 11:5 relocates/re-points the MT's opening negative לֹא (lo', "not") to the end of the **immediately preceding verse, Hosea 11:4**, where it is read as לוֹ (lo, "to him") functioning as an object marker. This is the LXX-influenced reading (followed by RSV/NRSV; surveyed by Macintosh, ICC Hosea 1997). The effect is to remove the explicit negation from 11:5, yielding "he shall return to the land of Egypt," harmonizing 11:5 with 8:13 and 9:3.

**Why:** Pass-3 audit of batch_05 entry 379 (commentary_fix) flagged commentary_out saying the particle attaches "to the end of 10:15 instead (reading 'in a storm... is cut off, no more')." Two errors compounded: (1) wrong relocation target — it is 11:4, not 10:15; (2) the parenthetical gloss "in a storm... is cut off, no more" is the *content of 10:15*, conflated with the emendation target. The broader point (MT negation; emendation harmonizes) was sound. Classified medium → needs_human.

**How to apply:** When a commentary discusses the Hosea 11:5 lo' textual problem, the relocation target is the preceding verse 11:4 (re-pointed לו). If a draft names 10:15 as the destination of the moved particle, that is a verse-reference error. Verify against Macintosh ICC or BHS apparatus. Note this is a defensible scholarly emendation, not a strawman — only the verse number was wrong.
