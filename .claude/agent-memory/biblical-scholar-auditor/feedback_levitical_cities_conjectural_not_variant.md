For the Joshua 21 vs 1 Chronicles 6 Levitical-city lists (Aijalon, Gath-rimmon: Dan/Manasseh in Josh 21 vs Ephraim in 1 Chr 6), set `textual_variant_involved=false`.

**Why:** English 1 Chr 6:66-70 (= Heb. starting ~6:54; note the Heb/Eng versification offset) names only Ephraim and Manasseh as parent tribes; Aijalon and Gath-rimmon trail under the Ephraim rubric because the "tribe of Dan" heading dropped out of the Hebrew (a lacuna, likely parablepsis). The Dan reading is restored only by CONJECTURAL EMENDATION from the Joshua 21 parallel — Keil-Delitzsch ("a whole verse has been lost ... cf. Joshua 21:23"), Ellicott ("Clearly there is a lacuna in our text between 1Chronicles 6:68-69"), and Barnes ("We may supply from Joshua the words ...") all confirm there is NO manuscript witness preserving an alternative Dan reading at this point. Per the task definition, a conjectural-emendation-from-the-parallel with no MS witness is NOT a manuscript variant.

**How to apply:**
- consensus=probable_harmonization is defensible here (Ephraim labeling = transmission artifact, not an independent factual claim); genuinely_disputed is the more conservative alternative, but probable_harmonization is within range and not an error.
- The "LXX of Joshua 21:24 variant bearing on Aijalon's tribe" claim is UNSUBSTANTIATED — the genuine LXX-region variation concerns the Aijalon/Elon name (same Hebrew אילון consonants) in the Danite town list, NOT the Dan-vs-Ephraim Levitical-city tribal assignment. Do not let a draft cite it as apparatus support for the contradiction.
- Verified real citation for the source-list/parallel-edition framing: Yitzhak Lee-Sak, "The Lists of Levitical Cities (Joshua 21, 1 Chronicles 6) and the Propagandistic Map for the Hasmonean Territorial Expansion," JBL 136/4 (2017): 783-800. Treats the two lists as distinct textual units / parallel editions, not a single-point MS variant.
