D. A. Carson's John commentary is *The Gospel According to John* (Pillar New Testament Commentary / PNTC), Eerdmans 1991. Carson did NOT write a John commentary for the Expositor's Bible Commentary in either edition. The original EBC John (vol 9) was by Merrill C. Tenney (1981); the revised EBC John is by Tenney/Köstenberger.

Carson's actual EBC contributions:
- Original EBC: Matthew (vol 8, 1984)
- Revised EBC: Matthew (vol 9, 2010)

**Why:** AI drafts repeatedly write "Carson in his EBC John" when they mean PNTC John — likely because the same author wrote Matthew in EBC and is associated with both EBC and the broader Reformed-evangelical commentary tradition.

**How to apply:** Whenever commentary text says "Carson in his EBC John," flag it. Cross-check the scholarship section: if it correctly cites PNTC John, the commentary text needs to match.
