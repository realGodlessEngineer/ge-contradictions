Brown's *Death of the Messiah* (Doubleday 1994) covers Act I-IV: Gethsemane (§§1-12), Jewish Trial (§§13-26), Roman Trial (§§27-36), Crucifixion-Burial-Empty Tomb (§§37-48). The work ends at §48 (Guard at the Tomb / Matt 28:11-15 in context of the empty tomb) around p. 1313. **Brown DM does NOT cover post-resurrection appearance scenes** — Mary Magdalene recognizing Jesus (John 20:14ff), the two Marys meeting Jesus (Matt 28:9), the Emmaus road (Luke 24:13-35), or the appearance to the eleven (Matt 28:16-20). For those scenes, cite Brown's respective Gospel commentaries (Anchor Bible 29, 29A for John; etc.).

**Why:** Pass-5 audit of batch_25 entry 481 had Brown DM Vol 2 pp. 1373-1378 cited for the Mary Magdalene recognition contradiction (Matt 28:9 / John 20:14 / Luke 24:23). Page range 1373-1378 falls within **Appendix II: Dating the Crucifixion (Day, Monthly Date, Year)** which spans pp. 1350-1378. Mary Magdalene recognition is not in Brown DM at all — it's covered in Brown AB 29A John 20 commentary (pp. 1000-1004), which was already separately cited in the entry. Removed the spurious DM citation.

**How to apply:** When Brown DM Vol 2 is cited with page numbers > 1313, verify the page range against the appendices (Appendix I: John 14-17 sequence; Appendix II: Dating crucifixion 1350-1378; Appendix III-IX: passion-narrative subjects). Brown DM citations for post-resurrection appearance content are categorically misattributed — use Brown's John commentary (AB 29/29A) instead.
