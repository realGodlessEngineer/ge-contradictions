When AI-drafted scholarship cites Anneli Aejmelaeus, "How to Reach the Old Greek in 1 Samuel and What to Do with It," in the *XIII Congress of the IOSCS, Ljubljana 2007* (ed. M. K. H. Peters, SBL 2008) — this is WRONG. The essay is actually in:

**Anneli Aejmelaeus, "How to Reach the Old Greek in 1 Samuel and What to Do with It," in *Congress Volume Helsinki 2010*, ed. Martti Nissinen, VTSup 148 (Leiden: Brill, 2012), 185-205.** (Chapter 9 of the volume.)

**Why:** The Helsinki 2010 volume is for the IOSOT (International Organization for the Study of the Old Testament), not IOSCS (International Organization for Septuagint and Cognate Studies). AI confuses the two series because both deal with biblical congress proceedings. The Ljubljana 2007 IOSCS volume does NOT contain an Aejmelaeus essay matching this title. The PAGE RANGE has drifted across audit passes (207-228 then 185-206); the correct range per Brill's chapter-level metadata page (verified pass 7) is **pp. 185-205**.

**How to apply:** When you see the "How to Reach the Old Greek in 1 Samuel" essay cited, verify (a) the volume — should be Helsinki 2010 (VTSup 148, Brill 2012), not Ljubljana 2007 (SBL 2008); (b) the full subtitle "and What to Do with It"; and (c) the page range 185-205. Beware that a separate IOSCS Helsinki 2010 congress also exists (XIV Congress of the IOSCS, SBLSCS 59, SBL 2013); Aejmelaeus's contribution to THAT volume was "The Septuagint and Oral Translation" — a different paper.
