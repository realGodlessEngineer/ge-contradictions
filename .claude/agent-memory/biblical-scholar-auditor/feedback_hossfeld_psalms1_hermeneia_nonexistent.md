The Hermeneia English translation of Hossfeld-Zenger's Psalms commentary has only TWO published volumes: Psalms 2 (Pss 51-100, Fortress 2005) and Psalms 3 (Pss 101-150, Fortress 2011). Volume 1 (Pss 1-50) exists ONLY in German (Die Psalmen I, NEchtB 29, Echter Würzburg 1993) and has never been translated/published in English.

**Why:** Critical hallucination: AI drafts consistently invent "Hossfeld, Frank-Lothar, and Erich Zenger. Psalms 1: A Commentary on Psalms 1-50. Hermeneia. Minneapolis: Fortress, 2004" with plausible page citations. Discovered in batch_18 pass-1 where it appeared in three entries (Pss 12, 14, 19). Fortress Press's own product pages and reviewers explicitly note "Volume 1 in the series exists in German but has not yet been published in English."

**How to apply:** For any Psalm in range 1-50, the Hossfeld-Zenger Hermeneia citation is a FABRICATION and must be replaced. Valid English alternatives for Pss 1-50: Goldingay BCOT Psalms vol 1 (Pss 1-41, Baker 2006); Kraus Psalms 1-59: A Commentary (Continental Commentaries, Fortress 1988, trans. Oswald); Allen WBC Psalms (selected); Ross Psalms (Kregel 3-vol). For Pss 42-50 specifically use Goldingay BCOT vol 2 (Pss 42-89, Baker 2007) or Kraus 1-59. The German NEchtB 29 Echter 1993 may be cited only if author seems to be using German sources.
