The Samaritan Pentateuch at Genesis 11:32 reads Terah's age at death as **145** (against MT and LXX = **205**). This is a genuine, well-documented textual variant, not a hallucination.

**Why:** It is a classic Samaritan harmonizing emendation: 70 (Terah's begetting age, Gen 11:26) + 75 (Abram's age at departure, Gen 12:4) = 145, so that Terah's death coincides with Abram leaving Haran — reconciling Gen 11:26/11:32/12:4 with Acts 7:4 (and Philo, *De Migratione Abrahami* 177, who also places the departure after Terah's death). The MT 205 is the harder/earlier reading. Verified via intertextual.bible Gen 11:32 SP comparison and the task directive itself.

**How to apply:** In Abraham-Haran-chronology entries (e.g. id 534, "How old was Abraham when he left Haran?"), if the commentary cites SP=145 at Gen 11:32 framed as a secondary harmonizing emendation (standard treatment: Tov, *Textual Criticism of the Hebrew Bible*), that is CORRECT — verdict clean. A pass-1 auditor in batch_06 wrongly flagged it as "high: fabricated manuscript claim"; that was a false positive. Philo Migr. 177 supporting post-Terah-death departure is likewise genuine. Do not flag either. See [[feedback_levitical_cities_conjectural_not_variant]] for the inverse (a lacuna that is conjecture, NOT a variant) — distinguish real attested variants from conjectural emendations.
