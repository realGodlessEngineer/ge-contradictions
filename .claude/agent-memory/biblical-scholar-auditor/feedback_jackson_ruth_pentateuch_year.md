Harmonization id 182 ("Did any Moabite enter the congregation of the Lord?", Deut 23:3 / Neh 13:1 vs Ruth → David). Named skeptic = **Bernard S. Jackson**.

**Skeptic is REAL and fairly represented.** Jackson is an eminent scholar of biblical/Jewish law (Liverpool Hope 2009-2015; *Wisdom-Laws*; *Studies in the Semiotics of Biblical Law*) who genuinely engaged Ruth against the Pentateuchal/Deuteronomic Moabite norm. So named_skeptic = ok, NOT not_real/strawman.

**BUT the work is MISDATED.** The cited work "Ruth, the Pentateuch and the Nature of Biblical Law" is actually the 2015 essay "*Ruth, the Pentateuch and the Nature of Biblical Law: in Conversation with Jean Louis Ska*," in Giuntoli & Schmid (eds.), *The Post-Priestly Pentateuch* (FAT 101; Tübingen: Mohr Siebeck, 2015), pp. 75-111. There is NO 2009 publication of this title (Jackson only joined Liverpool Hope in 2009; his whole Ruth-vs-Pentateuch cluster is 2015-2017, incl. "Ruth and Ezra-Nehemiah in Dialogue" and "Law and Narrative in the Book of Ruth: A Syntagmatic Reading," JLA Studies XXVII, 2017).
