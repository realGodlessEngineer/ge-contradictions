Marcus, Joel. *Mark 8-16: A New Translation with Introduction and Commentary*, 2009 (Yale University Press) should be cited as **AYB 27A**, not "AB 27A".

**Why:** Yale University Press acquired the Anchor Bible series from Doubleday in 2007 and rebranded it as "Anchor Yale Bible" (AYB). All AB volumes published 2007 and later are technically AYB volumes. Marcus's first volume (Mark 1-8, AB 27, 2000) was pre-rebrand and correctly cited as AB 27 with Doubleday imprint; his second volume (Mark 8-16, 2009) is post-rebrand and should be AYB 27A with Yale University Press imprint.

**How to apply:** Any AB/AYB citation published 2007 or later needs "AYB" (not "AB"). Common pipeline-relevant cases: Marcus AYB 27A 2009 (Mark 8-16), Meier *Marginal Jew* Vol 4 (2009) and Vol 5 (2016) = AYB Reference Library, Bovon Hermeneia is not AB so not affected, Fitzmyer AB 28/28A (1981/1985) and Brown AB 29/29A (1966/1970) are pre-rebrand so correctly cite as AB.

Reference: Yale UP catalog page for Marcus AYB 27A explicitly bears "Anchor Yale Bible" series branding; all Anchor volumes post-2007 use AYB designation.
