- **1 Esdras witness-split fabrication**: AI drafts fabricate "third number"/alternate-witness claims for 1 Esd 5 family-roll figures — verify actual manuscript readings before accepting a claimed textual variant.
- **1 Esdras multiple families per verse (false positive)**: 1 Esd 5:13 and 5:14 each legitimately list SEVERAL families in one verse — don't flag "same verse, two families" as an error.
- **Adonikam-Irenaeus misattribution**: Irenaeus, *Against Heresies* 5.28-30, discusses 666 but does NOT link it to Adonikam; a claimed "637 in some witnesses" variant for 1 Esd 5:14 is also fabricated.
- **Josephus harmonization misattribution**: Josephus, *Antiquities* 7.243, records the pillar tradition but does NOT explicitly state that Absalom's sons died young — that claim is rabbinic, not Josephan.
- **Josephus 8.249 scope guard**: Josephus connects Maachah to Absalom+Tamar but NOT to Uriel/a granddaughter — false-positive guard for entry 273 (don't over-attribute the Uriel link to Josephus).
- **Zechariah son of Bareis role-inversion**: Josephus's Bareis was killed BY the Zealots, not a Zealot leader himself — caught pass-9 batch_16.
- **Syrian legates pre-Quirinius gap**: the correct roster is Sentius Saturninus / Varus / Volusius Saturninus — don't duplicate "Sentius" and "Saturninus" as two separate people.
- **Augustine dual-reckoning misattribution**: the Roman-civil-time-vs-Jewish-time reconciliation hypothesis for the crucifixion hour discrepancy is Westcott's (1881), NOT Augustine's — Augustine's actual solution is a two-stage Jewish-clamor/Roman-action reading. Don't attribute the wrong harmonization to the wrong (older, more authoritative-sounding) father.
