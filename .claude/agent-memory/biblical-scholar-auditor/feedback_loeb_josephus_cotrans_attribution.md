name: Loeb Josephus Vol V (Antiquities V-VIII) co-translator attribution
description: Loeb Vol V (Antiquities Books 5-8, 1934) must credit both Thackeray AND Marcus, not Marcus alone
type: feedback
---

When AI-drafted scholarship cites Josephus, Antiquities Book 7 from the Loeb Classical Library 1934 edition, attribution must be: "trans. H. St. J. Thackeray and Ralph Marcus (Harvard, 1934)" — NOT solely "by Ralph Marcus."

**Why:** Loeb Vol. V (LCL 281, Books V-VIII, 1934) was a co-translation credited to both Thackeray (who died before publication) and Marcus on the title page. Cambridge Classical Review and Internet Archive title page both confirm dual credit. Only Loeb Vols. VI-VIII (Antiquities IX-XX) are sole-Marcus translations. AI drafts default to "by Marcus" because Marcus completed the project after Thackeray's death, but the published volume bears both names.

**How to apply:** Any time the canonical text quotes Josephus from Antiquities Books 5-8 via Loeb 1934, verify the credit line reads "trans. H. St. J. Thackeray and Ralph Marcus." For Books 1-4 (Vol. IV, 1930), credit is Thackeray alone. For Books 9-20 (Vols. VI-X), credit is Marcus alone (with some volumes having additional translators for later books).
