When NT entries discuss the Quirinius/Luke 2:2 chronology problem and name the Syrian legates who governed Syria during the "gap" (between roughly 9 BCE and 6 CE), use this verified list:

**Pre-Quirinius Syrian legates (approximate dates):**
- **C. Sentius Saturninus** (legatus pro praetore of Syria ~9-6 BCE)
- **P. Quinctilius Varus** (legatus 6-4 BCE — famous for the Teutoburg Forest disaster in 9 CE, but earlier in Syria)
- **L. Volusius Saturninus** (legatus ~4-5 CE)
- **P. Sulpicius Quirinius** (legatus 6 CE onward — documented census year)

**Common AI errors:**
- Listing "Saturninus, Varus, Sentius Saturninus" — DUPLICATE: Sentius Saturninus IS the Saturninus. The third legate is **Volusius Saturninus** (different family).
- Listing only two names — incomplete; the gap has at least three documented legates.
- Confusing Quirinius's earlier military commands (e.g., the Homonadensian campaign) with a Syrian governorship.

**Why:** AI may collapse two Saturninus names (Sentius and Volusius) because both bear the cognomen Saturninus. They are separate Roman senators serving Syria at different times.

**How to apply:** When listing legates who occupy the gap, ensure no duplication of names. The standard scholarly list (Schurer, Vermes-Millar; Sherwin-White) is Sentius Saturninus → Varus → Volusius Saturninus → Quirinius.

**Verified case** (batch 20, entry 391): The cite originally read "the gap is filled by other named legates (Saturninus, Varus, Sentius Saturninus)" — duplicating Saturninus. Fixed to "Sentius Saturninus, Quinctilius Varus, Volusius Saturninus."
