Nahum M. Sarna, *On the Book of Psalms: Exploring the Prayers of Ancient Israel* (Schocken, 1993; ~312 pp.) is NOT a full Psalms commentary. It gives chapter-length treatments of only TEN selected psalms: **Pss 1, 8, 15, 19, 24, 30, 48, 82, 93, 94**.

**Why:** batch_07 entry 595 (Psalm 89, David's throne) cited "Sarna, *On the Book of Psalms*, pp. 152-160, on the literary movement of [Ps 89]." Psalm 89 is not among the ten — the cited pages and attributed content were fabricated (HIGH-severity misattribution). This is a [[feedback_block_scope_fit]]-style scope-fit error: real book, real author, but the cited work does not cover the passage.

**How to apply:** Whenever Sarna *On the Book of Psalms* is cited for any psalm outside {1, 8, 15, 19, 24, 30, 48, 82, 93, 94}, treat the citation as fabricated and flag HIGH. The fix is usually removal (commentary rarely depends on it; full-commentary alternatives like Tate WBC, Hossfeld-Zenger Hermeneia, Goldingay BCOTWP are typically already cited). Do NOT confuse this book with Sarna's JPS Torah Commentary volumes (Genesis, Exodus) — those are genuine full commentaries.

Verified-correct Ps 89 anchors from this batch (cross-ref [[feedback_hossfeld_zenger_psalm89_anchor]]): HZ Psalms 2 Hermeneia Ps 89 = pp. 399-415 (Project Muse 45967); Tate Psalms 51-100 WBC 20 (1990, 608pp); Goldingay Psalms 42-89 BCOTWP (Baker Academic 2007, 744pp).
