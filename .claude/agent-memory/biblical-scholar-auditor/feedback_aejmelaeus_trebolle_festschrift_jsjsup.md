Aejmelaeus's essay "Corruption or Correction? Textual Development in the MT of 1 Samuel 1" appears in:

**Textual Criticism and Dead Sea Scrolls Studies in Honour of Julio Trebolle Barrera: Florilegium Complutense**, ed. Andres Piquer Otero and Pablo A. Torijano Morales, **Supplements to the Journal for the Study of Judaism (JSJSup) 157** (Brill, 2012), pp. 1-17.

The series is **JSJSup**, not **VTSup**. Both series are Brill but they are distinct supplement series:
- VTSup = Supplements to *Vetus Testamentum*
- JSJSup = Supplements to the *Journal for the Study of Judaism*

**Why:** Pass-1 audit of batch_15 entry 286 found this miscited as VTSup 157. AI drafts conflate the two Brill supplement series because both publish OT/Second Temple festschriften.

**How to apply:** Verify Brill volume series codes against the Brill catalog page. The Trebolle Barrera Festschrift is JSJSup 157. The Aejmelaeus essay therein spans pp. 1-17 (chapter 1 of the volume).
