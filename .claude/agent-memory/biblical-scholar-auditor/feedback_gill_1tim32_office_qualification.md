When a harmonization-sweep deeper_learning pd_work cites John Gill on 1 Timothy 3:2 and
claims Gill "reads 'husband of one wife' as forbidding polygamy to all, not merely to
bishops," VERIFY against Gill's actual comment — that claim inverts his reading.

Gill on 1 Tim 3:2 (biblestudytools/biblestudytools Gill mirror) reads the phrase as an
**office qualification**: it "excludes all such persons from being elders, or pastors, or
overseers of churches, that were 'polygamists'; who had more wives than one at a time, or
had divorced their wives... and married others," paralleled to the Jewish high priest,
Egyptian priests, and Roman Flamines. He does add a passing aside that polygamy/divorce were
"not lawful nor to be allowed of in any; yet they were especially unbecoming and scandalous
in officers of churches" — but the THRUST of his comment on the verse is office-specific, not
a universal prohibition derived from the phrase itself.

**Why:** AUDITOR_SPEC deeper_learning guardrail requires pd_work to be a real PD work that
treats THIS contradiction AND that the note's characterization of it be accurate. A note that
overstates Gill's harmonization (turning an office-qualification reading into a universal-ban
reading) is the same false-concession/overclaim risk seen elsewhere — it makes the
harmonization look stronger than the cited author actually argued.

**How to apply:** Seen on harmonization audit id 27 ("Is polygamy OK?", 2026-06-23). The
pd_work was Gill on Gen 4:18-19 AND 1 Tim 3:2. The Gen 4:19 half (Lamech, tolerated lapse,

REAL work treating the contradiction → deeper_learning = **flag**, NOT missing. The flag is
for the 1 Tim 3:2 mischaracterization only. Recommend: drop/soften the "forbidding polygamy
to all" clause and let the Gen 4:19 tolerated-lapse harmonization carry the pd_work, OR have
the note say Gill disqualifies polygamists from office (his actual reading).
