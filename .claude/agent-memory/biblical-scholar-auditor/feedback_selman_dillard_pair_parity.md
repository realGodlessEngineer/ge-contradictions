In batches covering 2 Chronicles, AI drafts frequently pair "Dillard (WBC 15) and Selman (TOTC)" as a standard apologetic-harmonization citation pair. Scholarship lists often include Dillard but omit Selman.

**Why:** Dillard's WBC 15 (1987) and Selman's TOTC (1994) commentaries on 2 Chronicles represent the standard evangelical/Reformed harmonization tradition for 2 Chronicles. They tend to be cited together in commentary text but only one ends up in scholarship.

**How to apply:** When auditing 2 Chronicles entries, scan for "Dillard ... and Selman" or "Selman (TOTC)" in commentary — verify both appear in scholarship. Selman's 2 Chronicles TOTC = Downers Grove: InterVarsity Press, 1994. When page ranges aren't verifiable, use bare-volume citation.
