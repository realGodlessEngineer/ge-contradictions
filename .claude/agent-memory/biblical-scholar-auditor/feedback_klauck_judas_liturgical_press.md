Klauck, Hans-Josef. *Judas: A Disciple of Jesus*. Collegeville: Liturgical Press, 2006 — NOT Minneapolis: Fortress.

**Why:** AI drafts default Klauck's English publisher to Fortress (which carries his *The Religious Context of Early Christianity* and other titles), but the 2006 *Judas* English translation is on the Liturgical Press list (Collegeville, MN).

**How to apply:** Whenever Klauck is cited for the Judas monograph in Synoptic/Acts batches (Matt 27:3-10 + Acts 1:18 Judas-death entries), verify imprint = Liturgical Press, not Fortress. Same imprint trap likely applies to other Klauck Catholic-press translations.
