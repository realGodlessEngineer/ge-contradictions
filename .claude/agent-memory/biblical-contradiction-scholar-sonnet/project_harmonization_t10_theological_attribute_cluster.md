Ids 108 (God warlike vs peaceful), 110 (ark contents: 1 Kings 8:9 vs Heb 9:4), 116 (honor parents vs Luke 14:26 hate father/mother), 119 (law given directly vs via angels: Exod 19:20 vs Gal 3:19), 122 (enemies: Exod 23:4 vs Ps 35:8), 129 (does God tire: Isa 40:28 vs Jer 15:6/Exod 31:17).

**Why:** these "theological attribute" and single-OT-verse-vs-single-NT/OT-verse pairs are poorly served by the generic Gospel/Pentateuch-numbers-heavy PD well — the 20-source well is strong on Gospel harmonization and OT census/genealogy, weak on isolated theological-attribute or law/ethics proof-text pairs.

**How to apply:**
- 108: honest-absence. Troki (p.106, ch. on Isaiah 9:6 "Prince of Peace") argues Jesus can't be Prince of Peace given Matt 10:34 "not peace but a sword" — thematically adjacent but a DIFFERENT verse pair (Isa 9:6/Matt 10:34, not Exod 15:3/Rom 15:33) and the OCR is garbled ("Peacef" for "Peace?"); declined as too loose a match plus OCR risk. Wellhausen/Ingersoll/Paine all mention "man of war" only re: David (1 Sam 16:18), not YHWH's title.
- 110: honest-absence. Colenso (pentateuch-examined ~L2800) and Wellhausen (~L17240) both quote 1 Kings 8:9 "nothing in the ark save the two tables" verbatim but in a documentary-authenticity argument, never pairing it with Hebrews 9:4's three items — no well source engages the NT-vs-OT ark-contents tension at all.
- 116: AUTHORED. Ingersoll's Christian Religion Rejoinder (Gutenberg #38806) ~L11809 hits "Let the dead bury their dead. What is that to thee? Follow thou me." framed as evidence Jesus spoke of human/filial love "with contempt" — solid verbatim, thematically on-point even though it's Luke 9:60's wording (parallel saying family to Luke 14:26).
- 119: AUTHORED (only 1 of parityTarget=3 found — shipped 1 rather than force 2 more). Spinoza's Theologico-Political Treatise Part I (Gutenberg #989) ~L563-566 states "the old law was given through an angel, and not immediately by God" contrasted with Moses speaking to God "face to face" — a near-perfect textual hit for Exod 19:20 vs Gal 3:19, but the quote spans 3 file lines (hard-wrapped mid-sentence); verify each fragment separately with Grep since the joined phrase won't match across a literal newline.
- 122: honest-absence. Troki (~L7846-7867 and ~L8724-8746) discusses Exodus 23:4 (enemy's ox/ass) extensively but only ever contrasts it with Matt 5:43 "hate thine enemy" — never with Psalm 35:8's imprecatory prayer, which is the bundle's actual second verse. Different verse pair despite same book/chapter hit.
- 129: honest-absence. No well source discusses divine weariness/rest/refreshment (Exod 31:17, Isa 40:28, Jer 15:6) at all — closest hits (Ingersoll "never weary of killing") are about Jehovah's bloodthirstiness, not the weariness-attribute contradiction.

Lesson: a corpus grep hit on the SAME BOOK+CHAPTER as one of the two verseRefs is not sufficient — always confirm the critic is pairing it against something recognizably close to the bundle's OTHER verse/theme, not a different proof-text pair from the same broader topic (e.g. "hate your enemy" shows up as Matt 5:43 vs Exod 23:4 in Troki repeatedly, never as Exod 23:4 vs Ps 35:8).
