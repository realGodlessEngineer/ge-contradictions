When citing the major critical Jeremiah commentaries, match the volume to the chapter in tension or the citation is a scope-fit error (a recurring audit flag in the commentary_fix pipeline).

**Holladay, Hermeneia (Fortress), two volumes:**
- *Jeremiah 1: A Commentary on the Book of the Prophet Jeremiah, Chapters 1-25* — Fortress, **1986**, xxii + 682 pp. Covers chs 1-25. So Jer 22:18-19 (Jehoiakim donkey-burial oracle) → Vol 1 / 1986.
- *Jeremiah 2: A Commentary on the Book of the Prophet Jeremiah, Chapters 26-52* — Fortress, **1989**. Covers chs 26-52. So Jer 36 (scroll-burning, Jer 36:30 succession curse) → Vol 2 / 1989, NOT the 1986 Vol 1.

**Lundbom, Anchor (Yale) Bible, three volumes:**
- *Jeremiah 21-36* = **Anchor Bible 21B, Doubleday, 2004**. Covers chs 21-36, so BOTH Jer 22:18-19 AND Jer 36:30 fall in this single volume. (Jeremiah 1-20 = AB 21A, 1999; Jeremiah 37-52 = AB 21C, 2004.)

**Other recurring Jeremiah/Kings/Chronicles citations seen in these batches (verify pages per use):**
- Cogan & Tadmor, *II Kings*, Anchor Bible 11 (Doubleday, 1988).
- Brueggemann, *A Commentary on Jeremiah: Exile and Homecoming* (Eerdmans, 1998).
- F. B. Huey, *Jeremiah, Lamentations*, New American Commentary 16 (Broadman & Holman, 1993) — apologetic.
- Robert P. Carroll, *Jeremiah: A Commentary*, OTL (Westminster John Knox, 1986).
- Sara Japhet, *I & II Chronicles*, OTL (Westminster John Knox, 1993).
- Ralph W. Klein, *2 Chronicles*, Hermeneia (Fortress, 2012).

**Page-range caution:** Do not assert a Holladay page range for Jer 22 you cannot verify — the conservative, accepted form is the section locus (`comm. on Jer 22:13-19`), per [[audit-corrections-can-be-wrong]] (do not invent numbers). Same logic applies to any commentary where the entry-level scholarship string omits a page range.
