Worked TRANSFORM row, id 249, "Who did Elhanan kill?" — 2 Sam 21:19 (Elhanan slew Goliath) vs 1 Chr 20:5 (Elhanan slew Lahmi, brother of Goliath); same Goliath 1 Sam 17 credits David with. genuine_contradiction → discrepancy_first. Companion to [[harmonization-transform]].

**Named skeptic = P. Kyle McCarter Jr., II Samuel (Anchor Bible 9), 1984.** The IDEAL critical attribution for this row: McCarter argues 2 Sam 21:19 is the OLDER text and Elhanan was the historical slayer of Goliath, the deed later transferred to the more famous David; the Chronicles "Lahmi the brother of Goliath" reading is therefore a SECONDARY harmonization, not the original. This directly ASSERTS the contradiction (so it belongs on the discrepancy pole, unlike a critic who concedes — cf. the DiMattei-Pharaoh lesson). Verified via WebSearch (McCarter/Halpern "strong form": historical Elhanan, literary transfer to David). This is the go-to named skeptic for the Elhanan/Goliath problem; the broader Samuel text-critical family also = McCarter AB 9.

**Reconcile = K&D on 2 Sam 21:19, the textual-emendation harmonization.** The dominant PD harmonization is TEXTUAL: 2 Sam 21:19 is a copyist's corruption of the fuller Chronicles reading (Elhanan slew Lahmi, BROTHER of Goliath). Strongest single excerpt = K&D, but it lives DEEP inside the anchor block note_ref KD/10/21/15 (the "Sa2 21:19" subsection — display verse_ref still 2 Samuel 21:19). Two non-adjacent verbatim sentences joined sense-preservingly (argument premise + conclusion), both end on REAL source periods (no truncation marker): "The words of our text are so similar to those of the Chronicles, if we only leave out the word ארגים, which probably crept in from the next line through oversight on the part of a copyist, that they presuppose the same original text, so that the difference can only have arisen from an error in copying. … Consequently we must regard the reading in the Chronicles as the correct one, and alter our text accordingly; since the assumption that there were two Goliaths is a very improbable one…". Preserve the Hebrew word ארגים verbatim. Parity-capped to this ONE reconcile vs 1 named-skeptic.
