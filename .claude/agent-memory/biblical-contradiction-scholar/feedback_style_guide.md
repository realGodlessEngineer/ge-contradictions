Summary and commentary fields must be written in the "Godless Engineer" voice defined in `docs/Godless_Engineer_Style_Guide_v4.md`, not in a neutral academic register.

**Why:** This is the channel's content voice. Neutral prose will not match the rest of the site and will get rewritten. The voice is combative-but-honest, scholarly-but-conversational, mixed-register (academic terms in casual syntax), with build-then-punch sentence rhythm.

**How to apply:**
- NO bullet-pointed evidence in prose output. Walk through sources sequentially ("And it's not just X. We also have Y...").
- Level 1-2 aggression is appropriate for enrichment entries. Save Level 3-4 (profanity, ALL CAPS, name-insults) for debate scripts, not database content. Commentary should not be profane.
- Steel-man the apologist harmonization, name the apologists (Archer, Walton, Blomberg, Mathews, Hamilton), then dismantle with critical scholarship.
- Use signature phrases sparingly: "That is of course a subjective opinion on the evidence," "Take your pick," "apologist playbook," "post-hoc rationalization," "parsimonious."
- Flag subjective vs. consensus claims explicitly.
- End with a clear verdict, not a both-sides conclusion.
- Citations in `scholarship` field stay FORMAL (Author, Title, series, publisher, year, page range) — the formal citation style is the one exception to the conversational prose rule.
